Dhokla Sandwich

June 2017

Jump to recipe

When I got married I used to wonder at the dinner style of Gujrati’s . They normally eat only one snack at a time . If they make something like Dahi Wada they would just eat Dahi Wada , I being new to Gujrati culture could not understand this concept, but over the years I like this concept .

We follow this concept but perhaps twice a week . There are some dishes that are our favourite and our menu rotates around them . Steamed Dhoklas  are a hot favourite of the family . They are steamed lentil cakes and full of protein . I have made umpteen versions of these and today I shall share yet another version .

Sandwich Dhokla

Ingredients

Dhokla recipe

Tempering

  • 2 tsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1/2 tsp sesame seeds
  • Pinch asafoetida

Stuffing

  • 2-3 potatoes , boiled and mashed
  • 1/2 cup green peas, boiled
  • 2 green chilly, finely chopped
  • Coriander leaves, finely chopped
  • Mint leaves, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp red chilly powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/4 tsp rock salt
  • 1/2 tsp dry mango powder
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • Salt to taste

Method for Dhokla

  • Prepare the Dhoklas, following the above recipe,but while pouring the batter , pour a thin layer .
  • Once it has steamed let cool for a few minutes.
  • Using a spatula remove the whole dhokla cake .
  • Similarly make another cake .

Stuffing

  • Heat oil.
  • Add green chilly.
  • Mix all the spices and add to the oil.
  • Switch off the gas.
  • Since the oil would be still hot the spices will get roasted .
  • Add boiled mashed potatoes and boiled peas.
  • Switch on the gas.
  • Add coriander and mint leaves .
  • Sauté for a few minutes .
  • Keep aside .

Assembling the Sandwich

  • Heat a non stick grilling pan.
  • Place one cake and grill only one side.
  • Remove and keep aside .
  • Now place the other cake on the grill pan .
  • Spread the stuffing on this .
  • Now place the previously grilled cake , the grilled side up , thus making a sandwich .
  • Temper the Dhokla Sandwich by heating oil and then adding asafoetida ,mustard and sesame seeds .
  • Cut into wedges and serve .

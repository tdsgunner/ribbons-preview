Lychee & Strawberry Lemonade

Jump to recipe

With peak summers one must incorporate lemon juice in daily diet . Now one definitely gets sick of the same Lemonade everyday , so here is a Litchi and Strawberry Lemonade which shall refresh you in this heat . Even otherwise it is an excellent mocktail which can be served for dinners and parties .

At present Litchi is in season so one can use fresh Litchi juice , but when not in season use the tetra pack . I have used fresh Litchi and frozen strawberries .

This is my post for Day 2 under Summer Coolers.

Litchi and Strawberry Lemonade

Ingredients

  • 15 – 20 litchi , pitted
  • 1/4 cup strawberry
  • 400 ml water
  • 2 tbsp sugar syrup
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  • Combine litchi , strawberry and a couple of ice cubes in a blender, pulse till well blended .
  • Add sugar syrup , lemon juice and water , blend again or just mix .
  • Serve chilled.

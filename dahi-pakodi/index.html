Dahi Pakodi

February 2018 15 min

Jump to recipe

Dahi Pakodi is my choice for Day 3 under Recipes with three ingredients theme . I have posted Lachko Daal and Maad for Day 1 and Day 2 . All three recipes belong to Rajasthani Cuisine and were a part of the Rajasthani Thali that I posted for Protein Rich Mega Marathon .

Pakodi is a simple Besan pakoda which is quite small in size . We simply add salt and chilly to the batter and fry mini pakodas, these are then immersed into water to make them super soft .

Whipped curd is seasoned with chilly and cumin and then these pakodis are added . You could call these as another version of Dahi Wada , but here the whipped curd is not as thick as the one we use for Dahi Wada .

Chickpea flour and curd are the two main ingredients used in this recipe , strange some recipes with very few ingredients also taste fantastic .

Dahi Pakodi

Ingredients

Pakodi

1/2 cup chickpea flour
1/8 tsp red chilly powder
Salt to taste

Dahi

1 cup whipped curd
1/8 tsp red chilly powder
1/8 tsp roasted cumin powder
Salt to taste

Method

Add red chilly powder and salt to the flour and bind a soft batter, consistency of pakora .
Heat oil .
Add a teaspoon of hot oil into the batter and mix well .
Drop spoonfuls of batter into the hot oil and deep fry till golden .
Drain on a kitchen towel .
Immerse them in water for 10 minutes .
Whip the curd and add red chilly powder , cumin powder and salt .
Add 1/2 cup water and mix well .
Gently squeeze out the water from the pakodis and add them to the whipped curd .
Garnish with coriander leaves .

Dahi Pakodi

Prep5 min
Cook10 min
Total15 min

Ingredients

Pakodi

Dahi

Method

  1. Add red chilly powder and salt to the flour and bind a soft batter, consistency of pakora .
  2. Heat oil .
  3. Add a teaspoon of hot oil into the batter and mix well .
  4. Drop spoonfuls of batter into the hot oil and deep fry till golden .
  5. Drain on a kitchen towel .
  6. Immerse them in water for 10 minutes .
  7. Whip the curd and add red chilly powder , cumin powder and salt .
  8. Add 1/2 cup water and mix well .
  9. Gently squeeze out the water from the pakodis and add them to the whipped curd .
  10. Garnish with coriander leaves .

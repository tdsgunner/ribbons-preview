Double Chocolate Chip Cookies

December 2017 20 minEggless

Jump to recipe

Cookies are an all time favourite with kids , and if they are chocolate , it is a feast and if double chocolate then be assured they will vanish in seconds .

I baked these Double Chocolate Cookies which were spot on hit my grandkids . I used our regular chapati flour , the whole wheat flour but of course like I said before you could use all purpose flour or wheat , it really doesn’t change the taste . The texture surely changes a little but kids can’t make that out , they are happy that they have a jar of chocolate cookies .

Double Chocolate Cookies

Ingredients

1 cup APF
1/4 cup cocoa powder
3/4 cup sugar , powdered
1/3 cup oil
1/4 cup milk
1/2 tsp baking powder
Chocolate chips

Method

Mix the flour,cocoa powder, sugar and baking soda in a bowl.
Add oil , milk and mix well to form a dough, If it is very gooey, add another tablespoon of flour.
Add in the chocolate chips and mix .
Roll out small balls of the dough .
Place them a little apart as the cookies will spread on baking.
Press gently with a fork .
Bake in a preheated oven at 180 degree C for 10 minutes.
time will vary on the size and thickness of the cookies.
Remove from the oven once the sides are crisp. Remove to a cooling rack and once completely cool serve / store in an air tight container.
The Cookies will be very soft to touch but will harden on cooling.
These cookies are a little soft.So if required bake a couple of minutes longer but care not to burn them.

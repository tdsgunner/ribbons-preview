Scrambled Cottage Cheese

September 2015 Parties

Jump to recipe

Eggs are an important part of breakfast in most countries. The Costo Rico breakfast has a combination where they serve Gallo Pinto with scrambled eggs. Since I could not cook eggs I found its substitute with cottage cheese. I made scrambled cottage cheese and it got done in a jiffy, looked just perfect and tasted fantastic.

I made absolutely fresh cottage cheese and once the milk curdled I just strained it . I wanted it to be a little moist so did not make a block. 

Coming to the recipe

For Cottage Cheese

Ingredients

  • 500 ml milk
  • 1 cup curd
  • 1 tsp vinegar

Method

  • Heat the milk till it reaches the boiling point.
  • Add curd and vinegar.
  • It will curdle immediately.
  • Strain on a cheese cloth.
  • Use after 4-5 minutes.
  • You could reserve the water to bind dough.
  • Scrambled Cheese

Ingredients

  • I/2 onion
  • 1/4 capsicum
  • 1 green chilly
  • 1 tomato puréed
  • Prepared cottage cheese
  • Pinch turmeric
  • Salt to taste

Method

  • Heat a tbsp of oil.
  • Add onion, capsicum and green chilly.
  • Saute for a minute and add tomatoes.
  • Let cook till the moisture dries.
  • Add salt, turmeric and cottage cheese.
  • Mix well and serve .

No Bake Oats Cookies

October 2017 15 minEgglessKids' Favourites

Jump to recipe
  1. We just finished a month long marathon where we posted Protein Rich Recipes , September definitely is a hectic month with so many blogs to read , but a month where we get to see so many recipes related to just one theme .

We have begun with October and we are back to our regular 3 day marathons . For the first week I have chosen the theme Cookies for your Sweet Tooth and today’s cookie is a No Bake Cookie.

I have never been fond of oats as such , but I love them in cookies , ladoos or energy bars . These cookies just need to be mixed and set , it is as simple as that , but of course you need to wait for them to set .Actually if you do not want to wait , you can just finish the bowl with a spoon .  These cookies make a great gift and kids really like them.

No Bake Oat meal Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 4 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 11/2 tsp vanilla essence
  • 3-31/2 cups quick rolling oats

Method

  • Add butter, sugar, milk, cocoa powder in a pan .
  • Bring to a rolling boil .
  • Boil just for a minute.
  • Stir in the peanut butter , vanilla essence and the oats.
  • Line a tray with parchment paper.
  • Take a spoonful and drop on the parchment paper .
  • Gently pat with hands.
  • Let cool to set.
  • Wrap them in wrappers and store them in an airtight container .
  • Makes around 20 depending on the size .

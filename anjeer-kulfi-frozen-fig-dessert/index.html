Anjeer Kulfi / Frozen Fig Dessert

July 2017 4 hr 15 minFestivals

Jump to recipe

Kulfis are a hit in our house and I love experimenting with them . I love trying kulfis with different sweets and mithais too . This time I used the Nutty Fig Fudge. The fudge is sugar free , it has the natural sugar of figs , though I did add a tbsp of sugar because of milk.

I did not add any nuts as the fudge itself is full of nuts , you need not boil the milk for long too . If you do not have this ready fudge simply make a paste by soaking the figs in warm water and blending till smooth . In that case you shall have to add nuts of your choice . So let’s see how to go about.

Anjeer Kulfi

Ingredients

  • 500 ml milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Pinch cardamom powder
  • 4 pieces Nutty Fig Roll
  • or
  • 4 figs
  • 4 tbsp mixed nuts ( almonds, cashews , pistachios )

Methods

  • In a heavy bottomed vessel , bring the milk to a boil.
  • Add sugar and crushed Nutty Fig Rolls.
  • Cook on slow fire , till the figs have disintegrated .
  • Add cardamon powder.
  • ( Alternatively, add the fig paste and nuts and boil the milk till it reduces to half.)
  • By now the milk for kulfi will be thick, pour into kulfi moulds .
  • Wait till the kulfi is at room temperature.
  • Place in the freezer to set.

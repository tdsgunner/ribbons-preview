Roasted Spicy Nuts & Seeds

December 2020 1 hr 10 minHigh Protein

Jump to recipe

I

Roasted Spicy Nuts and Seeds are a mix of variety of nuts and seeds which are roasted and mixed with flavourful spices to make it a protein rich munch .

These nuts and seeds are ideal as an Edible Gift or for carrying in your bag while traveling , they make a perfect snack pre and post work out and they are ideal to munch while watching movies . Trust me these are addictive and you will be amazed the way they disappear .

I have a jar filled of these and it is kept in the TV room along with some other munches ,though. my hubby and I have a fixed portion of these . I carried these while we were traveling by road to Silvas . My architect and designer went crazy over these and I made a big batch to gift to them . Yes this munch makes a wonderful gift . Since I made a big batch I gifted them to my gym friends also . They were over the moon and now want me to start a business . So now you have to believe that these are super super addictive and delicious .

While making these make sure you roast the nuts well and each variety should be roasted separately as they all require different time . The spices are as per your taste . These are best enjoyed with a spoon , so that you get all the tiny seeds and the coarse cashew powder along with the nuts . Do give it a try and let me know , I bet you will love it .

Roasted Spicy Nuts and Seeds

Ingredients

  • 500 gms almonds
  • 500 gms cashews , broken variety
  • 100 gms pistachios
  • 100 gms sunflower seeds
  • 100 gms pumpkin seeds

Seasoning

  • 1 heaped tsp sesame seeds white
  • 1 heaped tsp sesame seeds black
  • 1 heaped tsp fennel seeds
  • 1 heaped tsp coriander seeds
  • 2 heaped tsp poppy seeds
  • Handful fried crushed curry leaves
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp cumin seed powder
  • 1 tsp yellow / red chilly powder
  • 1 tsp dry mango powder
  • 1/2 tsp black salt
  • Salt to taste
  • 4-6 tsp coarse cashew powder

Method

  • Roast all the nuts  separately either in a oven / MW or stove top .
  • Roast the seeds too .
  • Heat 2 tbsp oil and add sesame seeds,fennel seeds, coriander seeds and poppy seeds . Roast for 2-3 minutes .
  • Switch off the flame and add all the spices and curry leaves.
  • Mix well and switch on the flame .
  • Add the roasted nuts and seeds .
  • Add the cashew powder and mix well on medium flame .
  • Mix well till all nuts are evenly coated .
  • Check for spices at this point , you could adjust the seasoning as per your taste .
  • Remove and let cool before storing .

Nadiya Pachadi / Coconut Raita

September 2015 Parties

Jump to recipe

A few years back I did not know what Pachadi was, the blogging world teaches you so much. I could have never thought of making a raita (or pachadi if you would prefer) with shredded coconut. For the Orrisa Thali , I made coconut raita , though I made a very small portion. I served a tsp to everyone wondering and waiting anxiously to see the reactions of family members. Surprisingly everyone loved it and wanted more . Do I need to say more ?

Nadiya Pachidi

Serves : 4
Recipe source : I camp in my kitchen


Ingredients
  • 4 tbsp fresh coconut
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2  green chilly
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp shredded ginger
  • Pinch hing
  • Salt to taste

Method

  • Whisk the curd.
  • Add shredded coconut, salt and ginger.
  • Heat oil in a small pan.
  • Add mustard seeds, cumin seeds, asafoetida and finally the green chilly.
  • Pour the tempering over the yogurt mixture.
  • Chill and serve as an accompaniment.

Written for her blogging marathon series Buffet on the Table.

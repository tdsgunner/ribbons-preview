Date and Nut Choco Orange Ladoo

June 2018 15 minFestivals

Jump to recipe

Date and Nut Choco Orange ladoos are delicious sugar free ladoos made with dates , nuts , cocoa and candied peel . They have a citrusy orange flavor..

I made these ladoos a few months back when I was traveling to Chennai .These ladoos were meant to be my signature ladoos . The Fruit and Nut Ladoos . I roasted the nuts and chopped the dates and then realised that these were being made for Valli and Veena . I immediately changed the recipe and decided to use cocoa powder to these , I know Valli’s love for chocolates , even her kids they want everything with chocolate . It was only after I met Veena in Chennai that I knew she is not very fond of chocolates !

I added some candied peel and orange flavouring to these ladoos and while tasting the mix for these ladoos I realised that the candied peel tasted divine . I actually love candied peel and specially the one I get from Amsterdam . It has a amazing flavour . Fortunately my stocks is refilled as I have a nephew in Amsterdam . Valli will tell you guys as to how these ladoos turned out . To finish up I rolled the ladoos into vermicelli and nut powder .

Date and Nut Choco Orange Ladoo

Ingredients

500 gms dates, pitted and chopped
100 gms almonds, coarsely ground
100 gms cashews , coarsely ground
1/4 cup cocoa powder
4-6 tbsp candied peel

Orange flavoring

Garnish

Coarsely ground almonds and cashew
Chocolate vermicelli

Method

Roast all the nuts individually .
Coarsely grind them , but do not powder them .
Mix all the nuts , reserve a few for rolling the ladoos .
Mix the dates , nuts ,cocoa powder , candied peel and orange flavouring .
One needs to mix them well with both hands till everything is well incorporated .
You shall see a glaze on this Nutty dough .
Pinch out lemon sized balls and roll them into ladoos .
Mix chocolate vermicelli to the reserved nut powder .
Roll the ladoos into this and place them into a airtight container .
The ladoos shall stay good for at least a month , longer if refrigerated.

Paani Puri Shots

March 2012

Jump to recipe

I have always been a Paani Puri addict  and this is very well known fact  in our family.Wherever I go ,my friends or family know that I have to be taken for this treat…not once ..but everyday till I am with them! Paani Puri is a weekly feature at home too.About a week back I was watching SK and these Paani Puri Shots.I simply loved this variation and so when Kalyani announced the Magic Mingle combo Whole Wheat and Corn I immediately decided on these shots.

For the paani

200ml . mixed fruit juice

100 ml water

To be ground in a paste

  • 4 ..green chillies
  • ¼ cup mint
  • 2tbsp .. coriander leaves
  • ½ tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and black salt to taste
  • Make a paste with the above ingredients adding salt and juice.
  • Mix fruit juice and water.

Add the prepared paste

Stuffing

2tbsp ..boiled corn kernels

1tsp .. finely chopped onion

Pinch chaat masala

Salt to taste

Mix all ingredients to prepare stuffing.

4 whole wheat puris

For puris

  • 1cup whole wheat flour
  • ½ cup ..semolina
  • Mix the two flours and bind a dough.
  • Cover the dough with a muslin cloth.
  • Let rest for 15 minutes.
  • Roll into a big chapati.
  • Cut rounds with a cookie cutter.
  • Fry the puris adjusting the flame .
  • (this will give about 80 puris depending on the size you cut )
  • How to serve
  • Take a puri.
  • Lightly make a hole  in it. Fill the stuffing.
  • Pour the fruity paani in four  shot glasses.
  • Place the puri on top of the glass.
  • Eat the puri and drink the water.

This water is really spicy and since it’s a shot gulp it down in one go…and then you feel the heat of the chilly ,but along with it the mild sweetnes of juice …the minty flavor…aw..it  is simply awsome.I find it really cumbersome to make puris at home so I buy them from the market ,but if time permits one can always indulge into it.

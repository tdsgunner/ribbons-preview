The Sinful Chocolate Cake

December 2017 1 hr 5 minEgglessFestivals

Jump to recipe

Day 2 under Christmas Dinner and I could not miss a cake .I would have loved to make Christmas Cake , but I already have two versions of it. Cakes have  always been  a must on Christmas when the girls were here . In fact we all were so fond of cakes that we either got one or made one . We just needed an excuse to eat cakes . With passing time everyone has become conscious and we avoid sweets . Juhi is the youngest and still very young so once in a while we do indulge into these treats . We do not want her to get over conscious at such an early age .In fact this cake has been baked by her, I just stepped in at the last minute to add those toppings and click pictures.

She scaled down the recipe to half . The recipe followed is of the basic chocolate cake , from Gayathri ‘s blog  , with a variation by adding a biscuit base to it . The cake turned out absolutely delicious , really spongy . The Hershey’s spread gave a Nutty taste and the kisses just added on to sinfulness .Talking of Hershey’s I have a real sinful pudding with it which is a total hit.

Hershey’s Sinful Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

Base

10-15 digestive biscuits
2-4 tbsp butter

Cake

1 cup all purpose flour
1/2 tsp baking powder
1 tsp baking soda
41/2 tbsp cocoa powder
3/4 cup sugar
1/2 cup milk
1/2 cup oil
1/2 cup curd
1/4 cup hot water + 1/2 tsp instant coffee powder
1 tsp vanilla essence

Topping

Hershey’s chocolate spread
Hershey’s kisses
Heart biscuits
Wafer rolls

Method

Crush the digestive biscuits .
Add butter and rub it in with finger tips .
Press down the biscuit mix into a loose bottom cake tin and freeze for 15 minutes .
Preheat the oven to 175deg C.
Mix sugar , flour , cocoa powder, baking powder and baking soda.
Make a well in the centre and add oil, milk, curd and vanilla .
Mix till all ingredients are incorporated .
Boil water add coffee and mix this hot concoction to the batter.
Pour the batter into the prepared baking tin which has been lined with biscuit crumble.
Bake for 40 mins or until a skewer comes out clean .
Let the cake cool.
Push the cake and carefully slide to bring out from the tin .
Spread a thick layer of chocolate spread over the top of the cake .
Decorate with Hershey’s kisses , biscuits or any chocolate you have .

Chenna Parwal

May 2012

Jump to recipe

For my nephew’s wedding I took charge of getting all the sweets from the sweet shop, and the chatter box that I am I started discussing the various mithais that this shop served.I saw a variety of parwal mithai which I found very interesting.These looked very attractive and inviting. I tried two of these and fell for them , ever since had been wanting to try these. So here I am with one of the variations. (yes, I grabbed the recipe from the mithai wala ).

  •  Parwals
  • 250 gms .. parwal
  • Pinch baking soda
  • Peel the parwals.
  • Slit them legnthwise, discard the seeds, keeping the parwals intact.
  • Keep a pot of water to boil.
  • Add a pinch of cooking soda and parwals.
  • Cook till done.
  • Remove and gently  squeeze out water.
  • Dip them in sugar syrup , leave it to soak for atleast 30 mins.

Sugar syrup

Stuffing

  • 150 gms .. fresh chenna / cottage cheese
  • 1tsp .finely chopped almonds
  • 1tsp . finely chopped pistachios
  • Icing sugar as per taste
  • Large pinch cardamon pwd
  • Few strands saffron
  • Mash the cottage cheese ,adding all the ingredients.
  • Check the sweetness. It should be mildly sweet.
  • Assembling
  • Using a slotted spoon remove the parwals from the syrup.
  • Make oblong rolls from stuffing and place them in the slits.

Garnish

  • Garnish these with tutti fruity and pistachio silvers.
  • You could use warq (silver leaf) if you wish.
  • Chill and serve.

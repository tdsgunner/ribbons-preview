Hari Mirch ka Sambhaar

August 2011

Jump to recipe

Condiment  as Wiki says is prepared food to make food tastier or taste different.The category is vast and while choosing my recipes for this segment I have had a tough time,too many to choose from!!the first recipe for this theme is Mirch Ka Sambhaar…no no..nothing to do with Sambar.These are sauted chillies,just to add that bit of fiery taste to your food.If you are not fond of hot ones you could use capsicum too.Infact it’s  totally upto you as to how hot you want this to be.

  • 1cup ..chopped green chilly
  • Itsp ..musturd seeds
  • 4-5 tsp .oil
  • Pinch hing
  • ¼ tsp ..turmeric pwd
  • ½ tsp dhania jeera pwd
  • Itsp .lemon juice
  • ¼ cup ..besan
  • Salt to taste
  • Heat oil in a pan.
  • Add musturd seeds and hing.
  • Add chillies.Saute’.
  • Add salt and spices.
  • Reduce flame .Cook on slow fire for 2 mins.
  • Sprinkle besan and lemon juice.
  • Sprinkle about 1 tsp of water.
  • Let cook on slow fire for a few minutes.

This sambhaar has an awsum  taste and it can be relished with any meal.It can be eaten with rotis,puris…just about anything.You can store in the frig for atleast a week.To make it last longer all you have to do is increase oil n salt.

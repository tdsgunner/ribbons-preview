Nachini Crisps with Mango Salsa

June 2011

Jump to recipe

Ragi / Nachini was a name that I heard very recently.Staying in Gujrat we are more familiar with Bajra.My hunt for ragi flour proved fruitful,I see that many people are getting aware of different grains and their benefits.Ragi is full of iron and calcium,it helps maintain bloodflow and protects against heart disease.

I tried these healthy Ragi Crisps which are absolutely delicious and low cal.I have picked up the recipe from Tarla Dalal’s Cooking &more nov-dec 2010 issue. 

       The changes I made were few…I omitted wheat bran(did’nt have it at home) and added my favorite herbs…chilly flakes,oregano and pepper.Instead of baking these I put them in a toaster and there they turned nice and crisp…it took about 25 mins.

I served these with Mango Salsa and they tasted awsum.Chilled Mango Salsa with healthy Ragi Crisps,absolutely ideal to munch on in this summer heat.

  • Nachini Crisps
  • ¾ cup Ragi flour
  • ¼ cup wheat flour
  • 1tsp crushed pepper
  • 1tsp oregano
  • 1tsp chilly flakes
  • 1tsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Combine all ingredients in a bowl.

Knead a firm dough

  • Roll into small discs.
  • Roast them in a toaster  till crisp.
  • or
  • Bake in an oven at 180deg for 15 mins.
  • Mango Salsa
  • 1 .large mango roughly chopped
  • 1 onion finely chopped
  • 2 .. green chilly finely chopped
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • Salt to taste
  • Corriander leaves
  • Mix all the ingredients and chill.

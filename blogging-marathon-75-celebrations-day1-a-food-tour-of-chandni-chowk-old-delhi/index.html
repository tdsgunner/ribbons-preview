Blogging Marathon #75 Celebrations. Day1 A Food Tour of Chandni Chowk, Old Delhi

February 2017

  • Day 1 The Meet begins
  • Date : 16 th Feb, 2017, Day : Friday
  • Color code : shades of red, orange , with black stoles.
  • Iteniary of the day : A Food walk Tour of Old Delhi
  • Wake up time : 6 a.m

  • Breakfast 7.15 am at Sai Villa
  • Grilled potato and onion sandwiches
  • Cucumber and tomato sandwiches
  • Tea/ coffee/ juice

8 a.m departure for Hauz Khas Metro Station

8.20 departure for Chawri Bazar from Hauz Khas.

Chawri Bazar

Chawri Bazar is a specialized wholesale market of brass, copper and paper products.Established in 1840, with a hardware market, it was the first wholesale market of Old Delhi it lies to the west of Jama Masjid in Delhi.

  • Our first food stop
  • Road side Aloo Parathas with green garlic spicy chutney and pickle.
  • The Paratha were made live..yes in front of us, hot Parathas !
  • We took just one and were tempted to have more, but No!

The stuffing of Paratha had boiled mashed potatoes, a bit of onion, green chilly , coriander, red chilly powder and salt.

Rs 30 / per Paratha

Walk 10 meters and a vendor frying hot Bread Pakoras.

Girls in red surrounded him, clicked pics and videos, took recipe and bought one plate of the bread Pakora.

Surprise ! It was served with Aloo ki Sabzi. I had never seen that before , perhaps a new addition to those regular bread pakoras.

The bread pakoras had a stuffing of boiled mashed potatoes,cut into triangles and dipped in Besan, a bit of all purpose flour, turmeric and salt, finally fried to a golden.

Rs 30 for 2 pieces with Aloo ki sabzi.

  • Next station Half way along Chawri Bazar, on the right going towards Jama Masjid .
  • Shyam Sweets
  • Bedmi sabzi
  • Nagori Halwa
  • Moong Daal Halwa
  • Mattar ki Kachori
  • Bedmi is a wheat flour Poori,a lighter version of kachori, served with Aloo ki sabzi and a crunchy carrot pickle.
  • Nagori are small Pooris made from semolina , served with Sooji ka Halwa.

  • We barely move out and we see a vendor selling
  • Daulat ki Chat
  • Can’t understand why this name, because it is not a chat !

Milk and cream are beaten together and made frothy, this froth is then served with powdered jaggery and some froth which has saffron. The powdered jaggery works as praline, gives it a amazing crunch.

Our girls- on seventh moon .

A small bowl for Rs .50

We walk through the narrow lanes of Chawri and go into Kucha Pati Ram, Bazar Sita Ram.


  • 9 a.m
  • Kuremal Mohan Lal Kulfi Wale
  • The shop opens at 9am, but it was closed.
  • One phone call and it opened right away.
  • The kulfis that we had
  • Fruit mei Kulfi
  • Mango , Anar, Orange and Apple.
  • These are whole fruits, cored and stuffed with kulfis and frozen.
  • Rs 200 per Kulfi.
  • Kulfi Julep’s
  • Falsa, Jamun, Podina Masala, Imli
  • These were water based, sorbet kinds .
  • Mango, chandan, sharifa.( these were milk based )
  • Rs. 60/ Kulfi
  • Rabri Kulfi Cream
  • Khumani, fig, Paan
  • Rs 60/ Kulfi
Trust us to enjoy these kulfis early morning !

  • We step out of the Kulfi shop and what do we see
  • Cycle wale Chole Kulche.
  • We took one plate and clicked it on a cart.
  • We thought of Rajani at that time, the cart was just the kind of board she would like.

These cholas are made without onion and garlic, just chole and spices, then enhanced with mint and tamarind chutney. They are packed in a large tin container and covered with layers of woolen fabric so that they can remain hot throughout the day. They are served with kulchas from the local bakery.

Rs 20/ plate

We half heartedly finished Chawri and decided to go to Chandni Chowk. The distance is not much but walking through the busy lanes in a group was tough.

Ę rickshaws come to rescue.

  • A brief halt at
  • Gurudwara Sis Ganj Sahib , we sat and heard the holy shabad and had Karah Prasad.
  • One of the nine historical Gurudwaras of Delhi.

It marks the site where the ninth guru was beheaded on the orders of Mughal emperor Aurangzeb for refusing to convert into Islam.

  • We continue with our food tour.
  • Just across Sis Ganj
  • Bhishan Swaroop Chaat Corner
  • Aaloo Kachaalu Chaat
  • Boiled potatoes, cut into cubes and deep fried, mixed with boiled cubed Kachaalu.
  • Spiced up with a special kala chat masala.

A treat by itself, there are more varieties but I observed from the expression that they found the chat very tangy and spicy.

Ram Ladoo / Gulgule

Yellow Moong Daal fritters, jazzed up with grated radish and a spicy tangy green chutney.

The best ram ladoos are available in Lajpat Nagar market, but we were short of time so had to make do with what was available there, though these were excellent too .

  • Kanji Wadas
  • Lentil dumplings dunked in fermented water, spiced up with chutney.
  • I guess you need to develop taste for this , most of the people liked it, and it is my favorite.

  • Kalmi wada
  • These are deep fried savouries made from Bengal gram.
  • Jazzed up with creamy whipped curd, sweet tamarind chutney and spicy green chutney.
  • These have kind of disappeared from the markets, and are available only at selected stalls in Old Delhi area.
  • Papadi Chaat / Golgappe

Natraj Dahi Bhalle

Melt in the mouth Dahi Wadas, these dahiwadas have nostalgic memories, as they are in the lane where my maasi had her jewellery shop.

  • The same place serves Aloo tikki, nice and crisp.
  • Rs 50/ plate
  • By now everyone was exhausted with walking and eating. So some friends took a break and sat at Haldiram.
  • Kids had their shake at Keventers, a renowned place for shakes.

  • We could not miss the Parathe wali Gali so decided to make that the last stop. Quickly went in and clicked some pics.
  • Pradnya got a couple of Parathas packed.
  • ( our stomachs could not absorb more food )

  • We come out of the narrow gali and a Cheela vendor was standing
  • The paneer cheela served with grated radish and carrots and chutney.
  • Sandwich burger served with chutney.
  • Two bread slices are stuffed with some grated paneer , dipped into a gram flour batter and shallow fried on a gridle.
  • This is then ripped open from one side and a Tikki is stuffed in.
  • Again this was just for tasting.
  • Rs 50/ plate
  • A bit of shopping and we decided to leave this bustling area.
  • Boarded Metro Chandni Chowk to Hauz Khas
  • Cabs from Hauz Khas to GK 2, Sai Villa

  • Ok are you guys tiered?
  • Some coffee? Tea ?

  • Archana, Apeksha, Pradnya and Valli off to shopping.
  • The rest gossiping.
  • Dinner time with some music and dance
  • We had a variety of junk throughout the day so I made a bowl of salad.
  • But food was called for from the restaurant, served with hot chapatis made at Sai Villa.

Left overs from yesterday too were served. I really did not look at that food and was very happy with my salad, which was a mix of cucumbers, apples, onion, cranberries and walnuts dressed with salt,pepper and lemon.

Ice creams follow.

  • 10.30 pm
  • Even though everyone was exhausted Gayathri baked a couple of cakes for the next day’s workshop.
  • A couple of us dancing .
  • Chaos built up !
  • No yeast!
  • Gayathri all worked up ” vaishali, how can you dance, I am so stressed ! ,
  • Relax baby !
  • I made a couple of calls but all in vain.
  • So will wait for the sunrise .

Lying down in bed realized that we missed the jalebis of Dariba Kalaan, where each jalebi weighs 250 gms or more with the juicy syrup dripping all over. One can’t stop licking fingers !

With thoughts of the day gone by and the day to come I slept off.

Come back tomorrow to  check what we shall do .

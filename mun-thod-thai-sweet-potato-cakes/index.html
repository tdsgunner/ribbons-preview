Mun Thod / Thai Sweet Potato Cakes

July 2017 35 min

Jump to recipe

I am cooking Thai dishes for the Oceanic Cuisine theme . Yesterday it was a cucumber salad , for today I have Sweet Potato Cakes . These are just like our Tikkis and they make a perfect evening snack .

The Tikkis have a very pleasing flavor , they are spicy , tangy , salty and slightly sweet . You can say typical Thai flavors . I served them with Sweet and Sour dip . The dip is sweet and tangy, the recipe for it shall follow soon..For now it is these Sweet Potato Cakes.

Sweet Potato Cakes ( Mun Thod )

Ingredients

  • 250 gms sweet potato
  • 125 gms potatoes
  • 1 tbsp coriander leaves
  • 25 gms toasted coconut
  • 1 tsp soya sauce
  • 1/2 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp all purpose flour
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2-3 green chilli
  • 25 gms sesame seeds
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Method

  • Boil the sweet potatoes and potatoes .
  • Peel and mash them.
  • Heat a tsp oil and fry garlic and green chilly , add to the mashed potatoes.
  • Add chopped coriander, toasted coconut , soy sauce, lemon juice , salt and pepper .
  • With lightly floured hands , form the mixture into small balls .
  • Flatten these and roll in sesame seeds, make sure the seeds stick well .
  • Shallow fry till light and golden .

Mango Vaghariya

August 2011

Jump to recipe

A sweet n sour chutney from Gujarat.It’s best enjoyed on summer nights wen u r feeling hot n don’t feel like cooking…just remove the chutney from the fridge or from ur chutney rack…n eat with khakhras..roti or baakhri its light n delicious.

  • 1cup .raw mangoes
  • ¾ cup .jaggery
  • 1tsp red chilly pwd
  • 1tsp dhania jeera pwd
  • ¼ tsp ..turmeric pwd(optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Vaghaar
  • 2tb sp oil
  • 1tsp mustard seeds
  • 1tsp ..cumin seeds
  • 2 whole red chilly
  • Pinch hing
  • Heat oil.
  • Add vaghar ingredients.
  • Add mango pieces.Saute for a few mins,add salt .cover n cook for 2 mins

Add jaggery .let it melt ,once it reaches one thread consistency add spices n cook for 2 mins

  • Store in a airtight jar.
  • Stays best for 1 month..but ofcourse u can easily store it in the ref for a longer period.

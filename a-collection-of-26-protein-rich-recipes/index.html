A collection of 26 Protein Rich Recipes

October 2017

We have been enjoying  Mega Marathons since a few years now . April and September are the two months when we post everyday , except for Sunday’s . We have different themes for these Mega Marathons and some of them are quite challenging , but definitely fun. They become challenging when a clause is added to these. We secretly wish for no clause but when given love it. Valli always comes up with some super ideas and the theme is decided after a mutal consent. Let me share some of the previous Mega Marathon themes .

  • April 2014 –  A Re -cap of The Indian Food Odyssey A Journey of 30 Indian States
  • September 2014 – Re Cap of Around the World in 30 Days
  • April 2015 – Re cap of Fire up Your Oven April Baking Mega Marathon
  • September 2015 – Recap – Buffet on Table
  • April 2016 – A to Z , 26 Traditional Sindhi Sweets
  • September 2016 – The Sandwich Festival…over 40 Sandwiches ! – Recap of Cooking Carnival 2016
  • April 2017 – A – Z Baking around the World – Re-cap,26 Egg less Cakes!

This September has been a great month where our team has been posting Protein Rich Recipes . The theme Protein Rich was decided a few months back and as usual I started with preparations right away . I divided my themes into five weeks , though the first week had only two days .

For the first two days I posted Kebabs , and served them with different flatbreads to make it a complete meal . The second week was a Biryani Week , where Protein Rich Biryanis were posted , the third week featured Salads and I made sure each salad was made with a different ingredient which was a high source of protein . The salads were followed by protein Rich Recipes with Nuts and Seeds . We all know that they are a great protein source . The last and the final week I chose to post traditional Indian Thalis , where I carefully selected a menu which had almost all protein dishes , yet complimented each other . This was my favourite week as I could showcase that our Indian meals , if planned well will give us a balanced diet and we need not indulge into those protein powders .

And now a recap of Protein Rich Recipes , September 2017

Kebabs

Masoor ke Kebab

Protein Rich lentil kebabs have a Mughal flavor and this snack can turn into a delicious meal.

Lucknowi Soya Kebab with Ulte Tawe ki Roti

Minced Soya kebabs, made the Lucknowi Nawabi style. These kebabs are full of protein.

Biryani Week

Biryani Cake

A Protein Rich layered Biryani, which is compressed to make a cake.

Baked Khichri Biryani

The humble khicdi turned into a Protein Rich Biryani, which is comforting as well as delicious.

Rajma Biryani

A wholsome Protein Rich Biryani coming from a Punjabi Gourmet kitchen, a must make.

Hyderabadi Quabooli

A unique High Protein lentil biryani from Hyderabad, the city of Nawabs.

Amritsari Vadi Biryani

Wadis are concentrated protein from sun dried lentil dumplings. These have been added to make a lip smacking Biryani, A delicacy from Punjabi Cuisine.

Chickpea Biryani

The Punjabi Chole Chawal made into a gourmet Biryani, which is simple to prepare, is a complete high protein meal.

Salad Week

High Protein Lentil Salad

Lentils and Pulses provide fibre and proteins and are low in fat , these beans make an excellent High Protein Salad,, which is more like a chat.

Moroccan Quinoa Salad

Power protein packed Moroccon salad is a healthy meal option, go for it , it has a medley of flavors.

Thai Freekeh Salad

A high protein salad with the super grain Freekeh, made the Thai style. A medley of flavors and a must try.

Fruit & Nut Yogurt Salad

Fruit and Nut Yogurt salad gives you a significant source of protein for a relatively small portion and tastes heavenly too.

Mexican Protein Packed Salad in a jar

Packed in a jar, perfect for carrying to work , picnics or enjoy at home this Mexican Salad shall be loved by all.

Tandoori Paneer Tikka Salad

Spicy tandoori paneer is sliced and served as a salad on a spinach bed with pickled onion rings. A uique salad with smoky flavors.

Nuts and Seeds Week

Spiced Curried Nuts

Slightly sweet, slightly spicy, these buttered nuts are sure to win many hearts. Beware they are very addictive.

Spicy Nuts & Seeds Mixture

Nuts and Seeds roasted to lend that crunch, and mixed with spices to give that spicy touch , A excellent Protein Rich munch for in between pangs.

Mixed Fruit Trail Mix

Trail Mixes are a wonderful source of protein as they have nuts which are high quality protein and are highly absorbable , making it easy for the body to process its nutrients , they have seeds and some fun elements too !

Mocha Chia Seeds & Nuts Truffles

Vegan truffles , with mocha flavour to keep you going for a couple of hours .

Apricot Energy Balls with Flax Seeds

Apricots, almonds, walnuts and flax seeds enrich this no cook energy ball, Perfect for breakfast or after a workout or just enjoy it as a dessert.

Fruit & Nut Ladoos

Indian version of energy , protein rich balls made with dates and nuts, flavored with cardamon, these surely are a hit with all.

All the recipes featured in Thalis are protein rich. How ever some dishes are not. They have been added to compliment the meal and make it a balanced , complete protein meal.

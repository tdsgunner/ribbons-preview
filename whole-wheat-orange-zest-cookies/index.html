Whole Wheat Orange Zest Cookies

April 2015 Eggless

Jump to recipe
  • Week: 3, Day  : 3
  • Theme : Baking
  • Theme of the week: Healthy Bars and Biscuits

Can you believe we are half way through with this marathon?Time sure flies!!

When it comes to choosing flavors, I normally choose orange after chocolate.For today I have some whole wheat orange cookies. I added some oats to make them more healthy. The cookies turned out pretty good , though I wish I had added some more fat, as they should have been a little more soft. They were crunchy, but lacked the moistness that I was looking for. I had made only a small batch , and they were over right away.

  • Whole Wheat Orange Zest Cookies
  • 1 1/2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1 tbsp corn starch
  • 1 tsp orange zest
  • 1/3 cup orange juice
  • a pinch of salt
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar 
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4tsp baking soda
  • Add orange juice, sugar and oil. whisk till the sugar dissolves & and everything has mixed well.
  • Sieve the whole wheat flour, salt, corn starch, baking powder and mix well.
  • Add the orange zest. stir lightly and then add the oats.
  • Mix everything with hands and knead to a soft dough, divide the dough into 12 to 14 small balls.
  • Flatten the balls and arrange them on a greased baking tray with enough space between them.
  • Bake in a preheated oven for 200 deg C for 20 to 25 minutes.
Let cool and store .

Note : I realized I had used oats, so should have added at least 1 tsp extra oil, if you are using only wheat flour then 1/2 cup oil should be perfect.

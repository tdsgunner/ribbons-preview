Strawberry Orange Rain

February 2011

Jump to recipe

The market is still flooded with the two most beautiful,health fruits.

Oranges,juicy n sweet with a tangy taste..with lovely colour n texture…n Strawberries with their gorgeous red colour n tiny yellow seeds piercing sweet n juicy the sweet n tangy taste both blending together give us a delicious drink…topped with soda water the effect is absolutely tantalizing that of a drizzling rain! hence the name Strawberry Orange Rain

A tinge of lemon has been added to give that tarty flavour n retain the rich colours.

Antioxidant and loaded with vitamin c..the fizzy fruity drink is irresistible.

  • Strawberries 1cup
  • Oranges .1/2 cup
  • Lemon juice I tb sp
  • Sugar syrup 4-6 tb sp
  • Soda water
  • Mint for garnishing
  • Crush the strawberries.
  • Remove pulp from oranges n roughly crush it.

Add lemon juice n sugar syrup

Put lots of crushed ice in u r glass n add this concentrate.

Fill up with soda.

Garnish with mint.

Experience the drizzle!!

Baked Potato and Broccoli Cakes are my Day 3 post under the theme Mashed.

Potatoes are something that I can have in any form . Actually I can survive for days , no actually months on them . It is because my mom keeps telling me that greens are a must , I have become conscious and try and mix greens along with potatoes . Today’s recipe is also with potatoes , I added fresh blanched broccoli to make these cakes healthy . Normally these cakes are fried , but I have baked these to make them more healthy .

Baked Potato & Broccoli Cakes

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes , boiled
  • 1 cup broccoli , blanched
  • 1/4 cup bread crumbs
  • 1/4 cup cheese, grated
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper to taste

Method

  • Mash the boiled potatoes .
  • Mix in the blanched broccoli .
  • Add the bread crumbs , cheese , salt and pepper .
  • Mix well.
  • Divide into four equal portions.
  • Make balls and then flatten into round cakes.
  • Spray a baking tray with olive oil.
  •  Place the cakes and bake till golden .

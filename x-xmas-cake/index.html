Xmas Cake

April 2017 13 hr 5 minEgglessFestivals

Jump to recipe

What could I choose for the alphabet ‘ X ‘ but for Xmas Cake ? Frankly speaking I did not put in the effort to search for something else . May be I should have but this made my task easy . So it is Xmas Cake for A -Z Baking around the World

Xmas cakes are popular all over the world and each country has it’s own version , they all have different names yet all are Xmas Cakes.I have two different Xmas cakes that I have already posted , you could check these here and here. I would have loved to make a  frosted Christmas Cake, but like you know no one likes frosted cakes in the family.

Today’s cake is a jumble of many recipes. I like chunky fruits so I chopped everything big , but later realized should have chopped them smaller .The nuts and fruits need to be soaked overnight in juice . People consuming alcohol can add some rum to it , but since I do not I used apple juice .

This cake had a very earthy look , the coffee and the dates give it that gorgeous look . Candy peel in any cake is my weakness and I am forever digging into them . A lovely rich cake.

Xmas Cake

Ingredients 

For soaking

  • 1/2 cup orange / apple juice
  • 1/4 cup dates
  • 1/4 cup almonds and cashews
  • 2-3 glazed cherries
  • 1/2 tbsp tutti  fruity
  • 1/2 tbsp candy peel
  • 1/4 cup currants and raisins
  • 3-4 walnuts, chopped
  • 1/4 + 1 tbsp brown sugar

Dry ingredients

  • 1 cup all purpose flour
  • Pinch salt
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp coffee
  • Pinch cinnamon
  • 1/4 +1 tbsp brown sugar

Wet ingredients

  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp curd +1 tbsp water
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • Vanilla essence

Method

  • Add sugar to the juice, stir a couple of times and then add rest of the ingredients under soaking.
  • Mix all dry ingredients.
  • Mix all wet ingredients.
  • Mix the wet ingredients with the soaked ingredients.
  • Now add in the dry ingredients gradually.
  • Grease and line a baking tin.
  • Pour the batter in the tin and bake at 180 for 40-45 minutes or till a skewer comes out clean.

Note

Soak all ingredients under soaking in either orange or apple juice.

These should be soaked at least overnight.

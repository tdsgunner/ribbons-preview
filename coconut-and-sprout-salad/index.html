Coconut and Sprout Salad

October 2012

Jump to recipe

This month’s Magic Mingle has really made me crazy.I have been thinking hard , infact I prepared a chutney but I did not enjoy it, was slightly bitter so I decided not to post it. For today I have made a salad with coconut, cucumber, carrot ,green gram sprouts and fenugreek sprouts. Should we get onto the recipe…well nothing much to it just toss everything together and relish.All the same here is the what went into the salad:

  • 1cup thickly grated coconut
  • 1/4cup .. green gram sprouts
  • 2tbsp . cucumber grated
  • 2tbsp . carrot grated
  • 2tsp . fenugreek seeds sprouts

Chaat masala

  • Lemon juice
  • Coriander leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Mix everything , add chaat masala and salt to taste.
  • Serve chilled.
  • I grated everything nice and thick, so it was crunchy, but if you want you can grate it thin or even chop it finely.

Since the fenugreek was soaked and washed three four times, it lost its bitterness partly.Sprouting makes it healthy.So a health packed salad for Magic Mingle.

Subz Mirch ka Saalan

February 2012

Jump to recipe

Hyderabadi cuisine evolved in the kitchen of Nizams.This cuisine is highly influenced by the Mugal style…rich and spicy.The herbs and spices used in the dishes as well as the method of preperation gives them these names.Mirch ka Saalan is one of the most popular dishes from Hyderabad,which is served with the ever so popular Hyderabadi Biryani.

For this Blog Hop  organised by Radhika of Tickling Paletes I was paired with Alaysha of schmetterling words . Mirch ka Saalan was her first post for new year and since I had book marked this long back I decided to make it.Yes,I made lot of changes,I used some veggies along with the mirch and omitted tomato .The original recipe is here.Coming to my version of this Saalan

  • ½ cup . mixed boiled vegetables
  • ¼ cup . tofu / paneer cubed
  • 100gms big green chillies
  • Tadka
  • ½ tsp .. musturd seeds
  • 1tsp . cumin seeds
  • ¼ tsp .. methi seeds
  • ¼ tsp .. nigella seeds(kalonji)
    Roast and grind
  • 2tbsp peanuts
  • 2tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1tsp ..cumin seeds
  • Other ingredients
  • 1tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1tsp .. green chilly paste
  • 2tbsp grated coconut
  • 2tbsp onion paste
  • 1/4tsp turmeric pwd
  • ½ tsp . coriander pwd
  • 4tbsp . tamarind pulp
    salt to taste.

    Heat oil.

  • Shallow fry the chillies.
  • Keep aside.
  • In the same oil add musturd,cumin,methi and nigella seeds.
  • Add the onion,ginger garlic and chilly paste.
  • Cook till light pink.
  • Add the roasted ground powder and coconut .
  • Add spices and salt.
  • Add all vegetables,paneer and fried chillies.
  • Saute’.
  • Add water and simmer till oil floats up.

Dahi Boondi Raita

March 2018 10 min

Jump to recipe

Dahi Boondi Raita is a accompaniment which is made with whipped yogurt and tiny gram dumplings . It makes a great accompaniment with any Indian lunch dinner menu and can be prepared in a jiffy .

Boondi Raita is one of the simplest of raitas and every family has their own version . I make this Raita in a number of ways but to make it delicious one must use fresh chilled yogurt and whisk it well . I normally use a thick sieve , so I get a uniform texture . The boondi can be made at home or can be store bought . The stores gave a variety of boondi , plain , masala , with pepper . Here I have used plain boondi and soaked it very well . I normally like to use the spices as garnish ,though they can be mixed along with salt .

A chilled boondi raita in summers is very refreshing and can also be used for topping the chats . It tastes awesome with finely chopped green chillies , that is if you like raw green chillies .

I had made this Raita for the Protein Rich Sindhi Thali as both yogurt and boondi are protein rich . I am posting this under the Special Theme for Day 1 .

Dahi Boondi Raita

Ingredients

1 cup yogurt
2 tbsp boondi
Red chilly powder
Roasted cumin powder
Black salt
Salt to taste
Coriander and mint leaves

Method

Soak the boondi in water for 10 minutes.
Whisk the yogurt and add 1/4 cup water .
Add salt and black salt .
Squeeze the water from the boondi and add to the whisked yogurt .
Mix well .
Sprinkle chilly powder , roasted cumin powder .
Garnish with coriander and mint leaves .

Ingredients

Method

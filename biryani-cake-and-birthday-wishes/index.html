This week I shall be featuring Protein Rich Biryanis.

Biryani , is a complete meal with rice and vegetables or rice and meat . Normally long grained rice is used to make a Biryani , where the rice is boiled separately and each grain should be separate . It is layered with a meat based or a vegetable based curry and then cooked on slow fire or dum as we call it .My family is very fond of these Biryanis and even I love them since it is a one pot meal. One just needs a bowl of curd or raita .

For today I have made a Biryani Cake . Do you guys remember me telling about the rice cake that my Mil used to make ? I had promised to share her recipe . Well since this is a protein rich theme , I have changed the recipe a little but followed the same concept .The Biryani cake is dedicated to my dad , yes it’s his Birthday today and I make it a point to post a cake every year . Moving on let me tell you about this cake . It has dad’s favorite paalak paneer, mixed veggies and most of all long grained rice . He never never liked any other rice .It was always Basmati rice.The rice is cooked in milk , the spinach layer has cottage cheese and fresh green peas . The other layer has tomatoes and mixed veggies like broccoli , beans and carrots . The Biryani is garnished with nuts and seeds . Now doesn’t it have a protein punch ,where we have milk, cottage cheese, spinach, beans, peas , nuts and seed to give us that protein punch and of course it is served with curd , yet  another protein.
So let’s see how this cake is made.

Vegetable Biryani Cake

Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long grain Basmati rice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 3-4 cloves
  • 1 stick cinnamon
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 big cardamom
  • 4 small cardamom
  • 3-4 bay leaves
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 cup milk
  • Salt to taste

Spinach layer

Ingredients

500 gms spinach, washed and blanched
2 tsp ginger garlic chilly paste
1 onion , finely chopped
1 tsp kitchen king masala
1/2 cup milk
1/2 cup cottage cheese, cut into bite sized cubes
1/2 cup green peas, boiled

Tomato layer

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 1/2 capsicum, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 large tomatoes , finely chopped
  • 1 cup tomato purée
  • 1/2 carrot , cut into bite sized cubes
  • 1/2 cup French beans , chopped finely
  • 1/2 tsp red chilly powder
  • 1/2 tsp Biryani masala
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste

Other ingredients

  • Fried onions
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Boiled broccoli
  • Roasted almonds
  • Roasted cashews
  • Roasted mix seeds

Rice

Wash and soak the rice for 15 minutes .
Drain all the water.
Heat oil in a heavy bottom pan.
Add all Whole spices , sauté for a few seconds .
Add the drained rice and roast till there’s no moisture . Be careful not to break the rice .
Add milk, water and salt .
Let the rice cook on full flame till 80% rice is cooked.
At this point there should be some water too.
Simmer the gas , cover the rice and keep some heavy object on the lid . I normally keep my brass mortar and pestle.
Once the rice is done keep covered.

Spinach Layer

Heat 1 tbsp oil and sauté the onions till pink.
Add ginger garlic paste. Sauté again.
Add blanched, puréed spinach.
Add all spices and sauté for 2 minutes.
Add milk to get it to pasty consistency.
Add boiled peas and cubed paneer.
Add more milk and cook for another 2 minutes.

Tomato Layer

Heat oil.

Add onion and capsicum and sauté for a minute.

Add ginger garlic paste

  • Toss for a minute and add chopped tomatoes , cook till mushy.
  • Add red chilly powder , turmeric powder , biryani masala and salt.
  • Mix well and add the tomato purée .
  • Add the chopped boiled carrot and boiled French beans.
  • Mix well and keep aside.

Assembling the cake

Take a round steel container .
Grease it well on sides and bottom.
Arrange cherry tomatoes and boiled broccoli flowers in a decorative pattern .
Spread a layer of rice.
Press this layer with the help of either a greased steel bowl or a lid .
Sprinkle some fried onions.
Spread the prepared spinach layer.
Now spread another layer of rice , pressing with a bowl in a similar manner.
Next layer the rice with tomato layer.
Again sprinkle fried onions.
The last layer is of rice .
Press the rice again .Since the rice is hot pressing will compress them and they will stick .
After this close the lid of the container .
After a while invert the container upside down on your serving plate.
Garnish with roasted seeds, nuts , beans and cherry tomatoes.
Enjoy the Biryani with curd.

Hash Browns

September 2014

Jump to recipe

Hash browns are a breakfast favorite with most of us. When ever we go on holidays I always pick up continental breakfast at the hotels. My first attack is on hash browns. I simply love them. There are so many ways to cook these, some make these with raw potatoes, while some make with boiled potatoes. Some add eggs while some add cheese.

I like to make with semi boiled potatoes. I normally do not peel the potatoes and put them in a large pot to boil. I prefer to use medium sized potatoes and boil them for just about 15 minutes. This is the version I served with my English Breakfast.

  • Hash Browns
  • 4 medium sized potatoes
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped ( optional )
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper
  • Parsley
  • Wash the potatoes well to remove all the dirt.
  • Put a large pot of water and boil the potatoes for about 15 minutes., these should be almost done.
  • Do not make them very soft.
  • Let them cool and then peel.
  • Grate the potatoes.
  • Heat a pan and add butter to it.
  • Add onion and sauté for a minute or two.
  • Add the shredded potatoes, salt and pepper.
  • Add parsley.
  • Mix well .
  • Once the potatoes have been mixed and coated with butter flatten these by using a spatula.
  • Let it crisp.
  • Flip over and let the other side become nice and crisp.

I served Baked Beans ,Grilled Tomatoes, Sauteed Mushrooms and Vegetarian Sausages or this breakfast.

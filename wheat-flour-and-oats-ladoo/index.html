Wheat Flour and Oats Ladoo

October 2019 40 minHigh ProteinFestivalsTravel Food

Jump to recipe

Wheat Flour and Oats Ladoo are healthy ladoos. They are enriched with edible gum and coarsely powdered nuts and melon seeds.

The Ladoos are full of nutrition, as these are enriched with loads of dry fruits and edible gum. The oats lend a wonderful flavor to these Ladoos. The flour is roasted on very slow flame till aromatic, this is a step where you require lots of patience. If you increase the flame, the flour tends to burn, so roast it very patiently.

While one is roasting the flour, the oats and nuts can be roasted in the oven on two different racks. This way you save on time. Edible gum is optional and needs to be shallow fried. Once all these preps are done, it’s just mixing and binding and you have a power-packed energy ball! You can enjoy it anytime.

These Ladoos are apt for any festival, I made big sized Ladoos and packed them individually in decorated earthen bowls. They make such a wonderful gift for the guest who is coming in. The best is they have a good shelf life and are ideal as travel food too.

Recently Ladoos have become a hit with me, and I love making these for all festivals. Some other festive ladoos that you can try are Paan Ladoos, Rose Coconut Ladoo,  Paneer and Gulkand Ladoos. 

Wheat Flour and Oats Ladoo

Ingredients

2 cup oats powder
1 cup whole wheat flour
1 cup ghee
2 cups sugar, powdered
2 tbsp cardamom powder
2 cups mixed nuts, coarsely pounded
1/4 cup edible gum
1/4 cup melon seeds

Method

Roast the wheat flour, till aromatic and slightly brownish.
Roast oats till crisp, blend them in a mixer.
Heat ghee adds the edible gum, fry, and transfer to a mixer and blend to get a coarse powder.
Mix the wheat flour, oats powder, and the edible gum powder and powdered sugar.
Add the mixed nuts, cardamom powder, and melon seeds.
Warm ghee and start adding little by little. Bind the ladoos .
If you find the mix dry perhaps you can even add some warm milk for binding.
Once the ladoos are ready you can serve or store.
Mine was good for more than a month in the refrigerator.

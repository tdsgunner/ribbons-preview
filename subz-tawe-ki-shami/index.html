Subz Tawe ki Shami

March 2020 35 minAir Fryer

Jump to recipe

Subz Tawe ki Shami are delicious ,melt in the mouth Kebabs made with minced veggies and spices .

For this week I have picked the theme Anything you can Snack On ! Now this is such a wide theme , one really gets lost ! So, I picked another theme on this and decided to make Kebabs for all three days . Three kebabs to snack on !

The Kebabs originated in Middle East , but definitely are a very popular snack in India . Normally when we say Kebab we always think of meat kebabs , but with increase of vegetarianism and vegans , a lot of varieties and recipes have come up for them.

While we had the hotel , the restaurant served a big variety of both veg as well as non veg kebabs . The chef was always eager to try new recipes that I gave him . We tried the vegetarian versions of famous Kakori Kebabs , Galauti Kebabs and many more .

Today’s Subz Shami Kebab too come from a non veg variety – The Shami Kebabs  ! Normally the non veg ones are made with minced lamb , here I have used minced veggies and binded them with chickpea flour . The non veg ones could be binded with flour/ eggs or even mashed chickpeas .

One can use any veggies to make Subz Shami Kebabs but we need to remember that while boiling or blanching them , they would have some moisture , and we need to remove that . Best is to squeeze them in a muslin cloth . The drained water can be used for binding the dough or adding to curries .

Once we have prepared the veggies we need to add potatoes , spices and the binding . We can make the kebabs on skewers or form patty or even make rolls . We can bake them , deep fry , shallow fry or even use a air fryer to cook these beautiful kebabs .

Drizzle some chaat masala and lemon juice and serve them with some green chutney and onions and you have a feast !

Subz Tawe ki Shami 

Ingredients

1 carrot , diced
100 gm bean green, finely chopped
100 gm peas
50 gm cauliflower
200 gm potatoes
2 green chilli
2 inches ginger
2 tbsp cumin powder, roasted
3 1/2 tbsp roasted gram flour (besan)
1/4 tsp black cardamom powder
1 tsp chaat masala
Salt to taste

Method

Boil the potatoes , peel and mash them . Blanch all the vegetables and process them together coarsely in a blender place in a piece of muslin cloth and squeeze gently to remove excess water.
Mix the potatoes and veggies .
Add the green chilies, ginger, roasted gram powder, roasted cumin powder, cardamom powder, chaat masala and salt.
Mix well , check for seasoning .
Pinch out small balls from the mixture and shape into patties.
Shallow fry or deep fry or roast them over charcoal.
Serve with mint chutney, onions and lemon slices.

Subz Tawe ki Shami

Prep20 min
Cook15 min
Total35 min

Ingredients

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes , peel and mash them .
  2. Blanch all the vegetables and process them together coarsely in a blender place in a piece of muslin cloth and squeeze gently to remove excess water.
  3. Mix the potatoes and veggies .
  4. Add the green chilies, ginger, roasted gram powder, roasted cumin powder, cardamom powder, chaat masala and salt.
  5. Mix well , check for seasoning .
  6. Pinch out small balls from the mixture and shape into patties.
  7. Shallow fry or deep fry or roast them over charcoal.
  8. Serve with mint chutney, onions and lemon slices.

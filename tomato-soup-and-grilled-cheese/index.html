Tomato Soup and Grilled Cheese

September 2015 Parties

Jump to recipe
  • Week : 1 , Day : 2
  • Theme : Classic Combos
  • Country : America

Soup and sandwich is a classic lunch combo. This can be ordered at any restaurant and the most popular soup is Tomato soup. As far as I think there is no pairing more comforting or better balanced than these two. We love this combo specially in winters. A hot bowl of tomato soup, with either a dollop of butter or some grated cheese, and a nice crisp grilled cheese sandwich . It sure is a perfect lunch. At times we love to add some fries too, but this time I wanted a perfect classic duo.

Here is a combo which might be very popular internationally, specially in American countries , but it is equally popular in Indian families too. So let’s get down to the recipes. This is the recipe that we follow at our restaurant and is a big hit.

Tomato Soup

Serves :2
Recipe Source: Hotel Nest

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 clove
  • 1 star anise
  • 1 big Cardamon 
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Small piece cinnamon 
  • 3-4 whole black pepper
  • I onion, roughly chopped
  • 3-4 cloves garlic, unpeeled and slightly crushed
  • 1/2 carrot
  • 5-6 large tomatoes
  • Few coriander leaves and stems
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tsp corn flour dissolved in 1/4 cup water
  • Salt to taste

Method

  • Warm the butter.
  • Add the whole spices, onion and garlic.
  • Saute for 3-4 minutes.
  • Add the carrot and Saute again.
  • Add the tomatoes , let cook till mushy.
  • Add water and let boil for 15-20 minutes.
  • By now the tomatoes would have well cooked and the flavors infused.
  • Strain the soup well.
  • Add tomato ketchup ,salt and put the soup to boil.
  • Dissolve the corn flour in cold water and gradually add to the soup.
  • The soup will thicken.
  • Garnish with oregano, chilly flakes and grated cheese.

Grilled Cheese

Makes : 1 jumbo sandwich

Ingredients

  • 2 slices jumbo bread
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 slices cheese

Method

  • Apply butter on one side of both slices of bread.
  • Place cheese slices on it.
  • Take the other slice , butter side down , place it on top of the cheese.
  • Grill in a grill toaster, until crisp and brown or till cheese melts.

This is a soup that we have regularly, but I had never made the grilled cheese, where cheese would ooze out. For sure it was a sinful treat.

Brownies

July 2012 EgglessKids' Favourites

Jump to recipe

Children love something to do with chocolates, today I have made some no fuss brownies.These are a favorite with grown ups too, quick ,no bake brownies are really yum .The nutty flavor of walnut can be changed to your favorite one..or you could add some tutti fruity for the little ones.Original recipe from Tarla Dalal, I have just altered it to my taste.

  • ¾ cup . dark chocolate
  • ½ cup . butter
  • ¾ cup digestive biscuits, crushed
  • 1tsp coffee
  • ¼ tin .. condensed milk
  • ½ cup walnuts
  • Orange zest
  • Chocolate Sauce Mix
whipped cream mixed with chocolate sauce

Garnish

  • Chocolate sauce mix
  • Grease a 6’’x6’’ square cake tin.
  • Combine chocolate and butter.
  • Melt over a double boiler.till becomes a smooth paste.
  • Remove from fire.
  • Add remaining ingredients and mix well.
  • Pour into the greased tin and refrigerate till set.
  • Cut into squares.
  • Apply a coat of the chocolate sauce mix.
  • Garnish with walnuts and orange zest.

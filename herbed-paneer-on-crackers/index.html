Herbed Paneer on Crackers

May 2012

Jump to recipe

The mercury is soaring as high as 44 !!..and the only place that I like is either the ac room or the refrigerator. When it comes to serving light bites I try and find simple ,quick versions.Something which can be done in a jiffy.This herbed paneer is real quick, tastes wonderful no  oven…no gas no oil..no guilt!  Can we ask for more?

  • Herbed Paneer
  • 1cup .grated cottage cheese
  • 1tbsp fresh curd
  • 1tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 .clove garlic
  • 1 green chilli / fresh red chilly chopped 
  • Salt to taste

For serving

  • Cream Cracker Biscuits
  • Combine all the ingredients in a mixer and blend till smooth.
  • Spread over a plastic film and roll into a cylinder of 1 ½ ‘’ diameter.
  • Refrigerate till firm.
  • Unwrap the plastic film and cut into thick slices.
  • Place on cracker biscuits and serve immediately.

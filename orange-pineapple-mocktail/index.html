Orange & Pineapple Mocktail

May 2019 10 min

Jump to recipe

Orange Pineapple Mocktail is a refreshing sweet and tangy drink which will refresh and act as an energiser .

It is a quick drink which can either be made with fresh juices or even canned ones . I have always preferred fresh juices over the canned ones as the fresh ones have natural sugar , while the canned ones are loaded with sugar . Of course if you are short of time , you can use the canned ones , there’s no harm having these once in a while .

Orange Pineapple Mocktail is a drink made with the combination of these two juices and a tsp of lemon juice . You need to blend them with some crushed ice and pour it into the serving glass . Lastly top the glass with some ginger ale or a plain simple soda . If you are adding soda then you might need to add a little sweetener . A tsp of sugar , honey or stevia , the choice is your . The Pineapple that I used was very sweet , so I did not use any .

Orange Pineapple Mocktail

Ingredients

1/2 glass orange juice
1/4 glass Pineapple juice
1 tsp lemon juice
Ice cubes
1/4 glass soda / ginger ale / sprite
Sugar / honey / stevia ( optional )

Method

Using your mixer jar , blend orange juice , lemon juice , pineapple juice and ice cubes .
Pour it into the serving glass .
Top with ginger ale or soda .
Serve right away .

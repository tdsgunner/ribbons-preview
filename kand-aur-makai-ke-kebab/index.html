Kand aur Makai ke Kebab

February 2012

Jump to recipe

I have already posted some of my favorite kebabs like Makhmali Choliya Kebab,Raajma Galouti Kebab,Raja Kebab,and Hari Daal ke Kebab in the previous marathons and this time I had to work on all new kebabs..incidentally the day I decided on Kebab theme  I got a lovely recipe from a cookery show( I only watch these shows in my office,which is ..once in a while!)The recipe sounded interesting  with sweet potato  and corn roasted on charcoal .wow!..this would surely have a smoky flavor.So here is this amazing Kebab

  • 500gms sweet potato roasted
  • 1 ..corn roasted
  • 1tsp .oil
  • 1 ..onion finely chopped 
  • 1tsp .. .musturd seeds
  • 2tsp . chilly flakes
  • 2tsp . parsley
  • 1tsp .cinamon pwd
  • 2tbsp .. bread crumbs
  • 2tbsp ..roasted chana dal flour
  • Few chopped cashews
  • Peel  and chop the roasted /boiled sweet potato.
  • Remove the corn kernels.
  • Churn these in the mixer for a few secs.
  • Heat oil. Add musturd.Let  crackle.
  • Saute’ onions.
  • Mix all the ingredients and make a dough.
  • Shape into rectangles.shallow fry.
  • Serve with  chutney and onion rings.

These Kebabs had an amazing smoky flavor since both sweet potato and corn were roasted on coal..they had a very mild sweet taste  ,but very pleasing and delicious with interesting blend of flavors.My son who is my biggest critic freaked out on these ..Honestly speaking I liked the Kakori Kebabs.…but I guess evryone’s taste is different and anyway the platter has to have assorted ones!

These also go to Srivalli’s Kebab Mela

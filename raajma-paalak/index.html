Raajma Paalak

Kidney  beans or Raajma  is high fat free protein,high in fibre and low in cholesterol.With a combination of greens and cheese it adds on to its nutrition.Here is a recipe which is full of proteins and vitamins.

  • 1 cup ..raajma
  • 1 cup ..spinach(finely chopped)
  • ¼ cup ..cheese
  • ¼ cup ..milk
  • ¼ cup ..onions(finely chopped)
  • 1tsp .ginger-garlic.green chilly paste
  • 1tb sp ..oil
  • 1tsp ..cumin seed
  • ¼ tsp turmeric(optional)
  • ¼ tsp red chilly pwd
  • ¼ tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste

 Heat  oil in a pan.Add cumin seed.

Roast the onions.add ginger garlic paste.Sauté for a min .

Add spinach..roast till shrinks a bit.

Add raajma n cheese n spices.

Toss a little add milk.Simmer a while.

  • Garnish with cheese.
  • This is my Indian version,which can be enjoyed with roti or parathas.
  • Now to make the same into conti version simply omit red chilly turmeric n garam masala.

Add more cheese…ok.if u r health freak..use cottage cheese and don’t forget to add some extra garlic n pepper is an absolute must!!a crisp slice of toast…n u r ready with a wonderful healthy meal!!

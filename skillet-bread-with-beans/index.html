Skillet Bread with Beans

December 2017 3 hr 35 minEggless

Jump to recipe

I have a friend who is a generation older than me but very fond of cooking . I am amazed at her skills and energy . Her grandkids dote on her as she cooks best of everything . Seriously . Incidentally we meet everyday in the gym and our topic to discuss is new recipes !

On one such discussion she told me about the Skillet Bread with Beans . It definitely sounded like a dish which would be appreciated at home . She refused to give me the recipe , so we decided we meet and make it together . So all gym friends decided to meet at her place for lunch and while the rest were jabbering we both made the Bread with Beans . Since all prep work was done before, it was a breeze to make the actual dish.

The bread turned out extremely soft and with those beans made a complete meal . There were loads of more dishes but the Skillet Bread with Beans was the star of the day and my Day 2 post for the Special Theme.

Skillet Bread with Beans

Ingredients

Bread

  • 300 gms all purpose flour
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 6 tsp milk powder
  • 4 tsp butter

Beans

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3-4 cloves garlic , chopped
  • 1/2 cup onion, chopped
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 cup boiled kidney beans
  • 1/4 cup tomato purée
  • 2 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1/4 tsp oregano
  • Chilly flakes to taste
  • Salt to taste

Other ingredients

Cheese

Method

  • Warm water and add yeast, salt , sugar, milk powder and butter .
  • Sieve the flour.
  • Adding the warm water mix , bind the dough .
  • Cover and let rest until double.
  • Punch down the dough and make balls and set them all around the skillet leaving  the centre empty.
  • Cover the skillet and let the bread rest for 15 minutes .

Meanwhile prepare the stuffing

  • Once the stuffing is ready place it in the centre .
  • Grate cheese over it .
  • Apply a light milk wash to the bread and sprinkle oregano , chilly flakes and some cheese.
  • Bake at 180 deg C for 20-25 minuets .

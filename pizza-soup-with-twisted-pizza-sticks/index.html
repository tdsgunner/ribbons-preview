Pizza Soup with Twisted Pizza Sticks

August 2017 35 min

Jump to recipe

I am working on the theme Make your own Pizza , a theme which is very easy and yet difficult . Easy , since it has so many versions like Pinwheel Pizza, Pizza Cups , Pizza Wontons , but gets difficult when you are off the basic ingredient like all purpose flour!

Anyways , I decided to make a Pizza Soup , and served it with twisted pizza sticks . I had the soup minus the cheese and sticks , but the rest enjoyed this cheesy soup . It definitely was a treat for young Juhi who loves cheesy stuff .

The pizza sticks are made from pizza dough, where the dough has been rolled, then twisted and baked. When you dip these sticks into this cheesy soup, you feel you are eating a pizza..seriously , no jokes. Try making it and you will agree.

So let’s get on to the recipe.

Pizza Soup

Ingredients

1 onion
6-8 cloves garlic, chopped
2-3 piri piri
Handful fresh basil leaves
2 cans tomatoes
4 tbsp tomato paste
Salt to taste
Pepper
Mozzarella cheese
Oregano
Chilly flakes

Method

Heat oil.
Add chopped onion.
Add garlic and piri piri.
Sauté for a few minutes .
Add the canned tomatoes and fresh basil leaves.
Cook till the moisture from the tomatoes is gone.
Add the tomato paste.
Cool.
Grind to a fine paste.
Heat oil.
Add some more garlic and piri piri.
Add the ground paste.
Add salt and pepper.
Add some water to get a thick soup consistency .
Boil it well .
Pour into individual bowls .
Just before serving add mozzarella cheese , oregano and chilly flakes .
Bake till cheese melts .
Serve with Twisted Pizza sticks made from Pizza dough .

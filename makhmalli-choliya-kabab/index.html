Makhmalli Choliya Kabab

April 2011

Jump to recipe

As soon as we hear the word kabab..naturally we think of some non veg bite…but I am sure veg kababs r equally..rather more delicious. Kababs have their origin from Lucknow well ..u can try u r hands on lots of veg ones… …Choliya or the green channa r  available only for a very short time n r supposed to b a delicacy so have tried a yummy kabab with choliya n paneer and yes in case u don’t get the fresh ones soak the dried ones overnite n pressure cook with salt…they taste equally good…n   u can substitute paneer with tofu .n now the recipe

  • 2cups choliya
  • 1cup ..grated paneer
  • 4 -5 green chillies
  • 6-8 .cloves garlic
  • 2inch ..ginger
  • ½ tsp ..garam masala
  • 2tb sp ..besan(gram flour)
  • Finely chopped corriander
  • Salt to taste
  • Garnish onion rings
  •                    Lemon wedges

chaat masala

  • Boil chana with salt.
  • Make a paste of ginger garlic n chilly.
  • Mix everything in a bowl.
  • (Plz mash the chana coarsely)

Now take a small ball of the prepared dough ,flatten it.

Wrap this around a greased pencil,press to give the kabab shape.

slide out the pencil.

 
  • Shallow fry.
  • Sprinkle chaat masala n lemon  juice.
  • These can b deep fried n yes u cud use ur tandoor too!!
  • These kababs r super soft…n they just melt in u r mouth.
  • Enjoy!!

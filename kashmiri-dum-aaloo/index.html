Kashmiri Dum Aaloo

August 2012

Jump to recipe

While preparing for this BM, I realized I had not posted the Kashmiri Dum Aaloo  recipe from my Kashmiri Thali.This is one of  the most hit recipes and though it surely is some procedure, but with a little planning one can easily make this aromatic rich gravy.You can even freeze the gravy , it surely is a life saver when one is short of time.

Gravy

  • Paste 1
  • 2 Onions, boiled and pureed
  • Paste 2
  • 2 ..green chillies
  • 1’’ .. piece ginger
  • 4 .cloves garlic
  • Paste 3
  • 8-10 .. cashews, soaked
  • 1tbs . melon seeds
  • 1tsp . poppy seeds
  • Soak all these for an hour and make a paste.

  • Roast and grind
  • 1tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp.. shahi jeera
  • 1 ..black cardamom
  • 1 ..green cardamom
  •  2 cloves
  • 1 ..small piece cinnamon
  • 1 ..bay leaf
  • 2tsp fennel seeds
  • 2 . whole red chillies
  • 1tsp coriander seeds

  • Other Ingredients
  • 4 .tomatoes, blanched, pureed and strained
  • 2tbsp fresh cream
  • 1tsp kasoori methi
  • Salt to taste.
  • Heat oil and little ghee.

Add the onion paste

  • Saute till pink.
  • Add paste 2 and give a good stir.
  • Add the pureed tomatoes, cook till dry.
  • Add the paste 3 and cook till dry.
  • Add the ground spices.
  • Mix well.
  • Add  water to get the desired consistency.
  • Cook on slow fire till a little thick.
  • Add fresh cream and kassori methi and switch off gas.
  • Place the stuffed potatoes in the serving bowl , microwave for a few minutes.

Pour the prepared gravy

Garnish

For Stuffed Potatoes

  • 4 .. large potatoes, cut into half.
  • Sprinkle salt on the potatoes.
  • Deep fry , adjusting the flame from high to medium, so that the potatoes get cooked and yet retain their original color.
  • Cool and scoop out the center.

Stuffing

  • ¼ cup .. cottage cheese, grated
  • 2tbsp unsweetened khoya
  • 1tsp .. kasoori methi
  • ¼ tsp red chilly pwd
  • Pinch ..garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Few cashews broken into small pieces
  • Few raisins chopped
  • Mix all the ingredients  and prepare the stuffing.

This gravy is very rich and creamy. It can be used for any dish tht you want to make special. Aloo , paneer, koftas, mix vegetables, anything would taste absolutely rich and delicious.

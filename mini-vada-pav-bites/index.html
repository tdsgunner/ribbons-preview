Mini Vada Pav Bites

November 2017 45 min

Jump to recipe

I remember my granny making real big size Tikkis when we had guest , she would say how can be serve such small ones , specially to guest . I came to Gujarat and here they always served small portions . Initially I wondered why but over a period of time I feel yes they were right . We all need portion control . Also bite sized appetisers are so much more convenient to serve as well eat .

Yes I am working on the bite sized appetiser theme and today I have Vada Pav Bites . I used mini burger buns , scooped them a little , just enough for a mini vada to fit in . Both green Chutney and garlic chutneys enhance the taste of the vada Pav . You could apply a little butter to the buns and MW for just 10 seconds before serving . These bites make excellent appetisers for a party .

Ingredients

 Mini burger buns

Batata Vada

Green Chutney

Lassan ki Chutney

Butter

Method

  • Using a mini round cookie cutter make a deep incession in the centre of the bun .
  • With a scooper scoop out the bun , it should be the size of the mini vada .
  • Apply a thin layer of butter on the bun .
  • Microwave the bun for a few seconds .
  • Drop a spoonful of green Chutney in the depression .
  • Fix the vada into the depression .

Top with lassan ki chutney

Ingredients

Method

  1. Using a mini round cookie cutter make a deep incession in the centre of the bun .
  2. With a scooper scoop out the bun , it should be the size of the mini vada .
  3. Apply a thin layer of butter on the bun .
  4. Microwave the bun for a few seconds .
  5. Drop a spoonful of green Chutney in the depression .
  6. Fix the vada into the depression .
  7. Top with lassan ki chutney.

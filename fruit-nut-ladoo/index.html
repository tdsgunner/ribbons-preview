These super energy ladoos are totally with an Indian flavour of cardamoms. We have dates , nuts and poppy seeds. You guys know my passion for Ladoos, they just added on to my previous collection of Ladoos. Here are a few which fall under the Protein Rich theme.

Today’s ladoos are with dates , which are a very good source of protein, and along with other nuts they provide you with roughly 5% of what you need for the day. They are rich in fibre and potassium too .

I had planned for a black bean energy ball , but when I made these for Raksha Bandhan we all loved them . I made a big batch of these Protein Rich ladoos and they were a big hit with all . You can scale down the recipe for a smaller batch .

Fruit & Nut Ladoo

Ingredients

  • 2.5 kg dates, pitted and chopped
  • 500 gms almonds, coarsely ground
  • 500 gms cashews , coarsely ground
  • 250 gms pistachios , coarsely ground
  • 250 gms oats , ground to a fine powder
  • 5-6 tbsp cardamom powder
  • Poppy seeds

Method

  • Roast all the nuts individually .
  • Coarsely grind them , but do not powder them .
  • Roast the oats , till crisp and make a fine powder.
  • Mix all the nuts , reserve a few for rolling the ladoos .
  • Mix the dates , oats powder , nuts and cardamom .
  • One needs to mix them well with both hands till everything is well incorporated .
  • You shall see a glaze on this Nutty dough .
  • Pinch out lemon sized balls and roll them into ladoos .
  • Mix poppy seeds to the reserved Nut powder .
  • Roll the ladoos into this and place them into a airtight container .
  • The ladoos shall stay good for at least a month , longer if refrigerated.

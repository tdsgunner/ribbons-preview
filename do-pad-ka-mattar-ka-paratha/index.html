Do Pad ka Mattar ka Paratha

April 2011

Jump to recipe

Paratha’s are welcome for breakfast,for us sometimes for lunch too mattar ka paratha is a total hit with my hubby.Winters is the ideal time  as u get real fresh peas but these come out real good with the frozen ones too…just need to overboil them.U can serve these with a bowl of curd or some pickle,but we relish the taste without any accompaniment.

Dough

  • 1cup wheat flour
  • 1tsp .. oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Combine all ingredients and knead a smooth dough.

Stuffing

  • 3cups ..green peas
  • 1 ..onion finely chopped
  • 2 ..green chillies finely chopped                        
  • Coriander leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Aamchur pwd to taste
  • Boil the peas with salt till soft,mash them lightly.
  • Add rest of the ingredients and mix well.
  • Roll out two small rotis,smearing them with some ghee.
  • Put the stuffing in the centre sprinkle some dry flour .
  • Cover with other roti.seal the edges lightly.
  • Roll out again( v lightly).
  • Roast on a hot gridle on both sides,using ghee or oil till crisp.
  • u ca see the pads(cover)being seperated
  • **
  • variation to this paratha
  • can mix tofu /paneer /boiled potatoes. /or for that matter u can even bind the dough with mashed mattar.

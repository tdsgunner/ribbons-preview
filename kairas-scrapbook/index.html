kaira’s scrapbook

March 2011

Scrapbooking

Two years back I wanted a handmade gift for my daughter’s birthday had already sent her photoframes..flower sticks then while watching some videos on you tube decided to make a scrap book for her.

She has a blog on cooking..so decided on a recipe scrap book..this was my very first one!!the book turned out awsum..but unfortunately the pics r not very good..:((

Ever since I got obsessed with this art. Here I  am sharing with u a book that I made for my grand daughter’s birthday.Her craze of Ariel…the mermaid made me choose this theme.

I used mount board as the base of pages so it remains sturdy n does not tear downloaded ariel images n tried to stick to blues n greens..though have used pinks too as this is her favourite colour.

The cover page .Ariel holding the big conch shell..i put a small diamond on her finger!!some embossed waves..shimmer paper n glitter bubbles  lots of ribbons n some charms..not missing the fimo beads!!

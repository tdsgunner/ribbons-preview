Oreo & Almond Carnival Shake

June 2019 Kids' Favourites

Jump to recipe

Oreo and Almond Carnival Shake is a shake with Oreo biscuits and almond carnival ice cream. This is a nice thick shake and is terribly sinful.

With the No-Cook theme, I wanted to make ice cream sandwiches. In fact, I did make them, unfortunately, with the summer at its peak, these sandwiches didn’t really work, the ice cream just went on melting. I tried to freeze but somehow I was not happy. It is then I decided to make a shake from these two. All I did was removed the ice cream and blitz the cookies then added the ice cream and ice cubes and gave it a quick blitz in the blender.

Ready to go! It is that simple.

These shakes used to be Dad’s favourite, he would enjoy them at Delhi’s Nirula’s, a perfect hangout for Ice cream and Shakes.

Today’s Oreo Shake was made for that little girl about whom I told you guys yesterday,  I had given her Nutella Shake.  She enjoyed today’s shake equally. I am happy I have someone who enjoys what I make and she’s happy as gets these shakes.

The Oreo and Almond Carnival Shake is my Day 2 post under No-Cook Dishes.

Oreo & Almond Carnival Shake

Ingredients

Oreo biscuits
Almond Carnival ice cream
Ice cubes

Method

Break the Oreos into small pieces.
Add them to the blender and give a quick blitz.
Add Almond Carnival ice cream and ice cubes to the blender and blitz again.
Pour into glasses and serve immediately.

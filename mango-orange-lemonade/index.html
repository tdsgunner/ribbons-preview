Mango Orange Lemonade

May 2017 10 minServes 1

Jump to recipe

With the mango season in full bloom, I had to make something with this fruit. This is one fruit that I can live on. I have seen friends posting Mango Mastani and I have been drooling over that, but I wanted to post summer coolers which  were dairy free.

So here is a delicious drink with mango and orange. You could make your own mango juice  or you could buy the tetra pack  but I prefer to use the fresh mango. Even the orange juice is from fresh oranges, though you have the option of tetra pack.

Add a few teaspoons of sugar and just a few drops of lemon, lemon juice depends on how tart your oranges are. If they are sweet then add more lemon juice. Top up the drink with carbonated water for a fizzy drink, but you could even use water.

Orange and Mango Lemonade

Ingredients

1/4 glass mango juice
1/4 glass orange juice
2 tsp sugar
1 tsp lemon juice
Carbonated soda / water

Method

Dissolve sugar in one of the juices.
Mix the two juices and chill them.
Add lemon juice.
Top with chilled carbonated  soda or chilled water.

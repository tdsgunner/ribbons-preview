Aaloo Tikki Wrap

April 2013

Jump to recipe

Weekend Cooking

When ever I want to make something simple and delicious, Wraps come to my rescue.This weekend I did not want to do any elaborate cooking, as it is really getting hot . I know my co bloggers, Valli and Champa would surely have something interesting on their weekend menu.Anyway I choose something light and simple.Well,I made Aaloo Tikki Wraps.For the wrap, I used the regular wheat flour roti, made by the young girl, who comes in to make chapatis every day.The roll and the salad were simple and got done quite quickly.

  • Aaloo Tikki Roll
  • 4 boiled potatoes, peeled, mashed
  • 2 green chillies, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh green coriander, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tbsp mint, finely chopped

pinch garam masala

  • 2 slices bread, pulsed in a mixer
  • salt to taste
  • Mix all the ingredients and make a dough.
  • Divide in two equal portion.
  • Make rolls,
  • Deep fry or shallow fry or bake.

I do not heat these in summers, but toast them up a little in winters.

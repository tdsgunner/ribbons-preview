Chocolate Truffle Cake

December 2018 2 hr 55 minEggless

Jump to recipe

Chocolate Truffle Cake is one of the most sinful , delicious , moist cake one can ever have . The cake is dense and a chocolate lovers delight .

For me this cake needs no occasion , give it to me any time of the day , month , year , I would be happy to gobble it down . A freshly frosted cake is my weakness , but even if it is chilled and hard , I don’t mind . I microwave it for a few seconds ! It becomes nice and gooey ! A truffle cake is heavenly and my favourite dessert .

This Truffle Cake was made by Juhi , she picked the recipe from Gayathri’s blog . This is one of the best cake recipe one can ever make , and almost everyone in my family is hooked on to it . Thanks Gayathri ! Needless to say the cake was a complete treat and while I am writing this post I am craving to eat some chocolate cake .

The cake has been frosted with Chocolate Ganache , which is silky and smooth . We did not cut the cake to add more layers of ganache , even though we love it . Just to avoid the extra calories .

Juhi is at present studying in London and how I miss that girl . We both are fond of cakes and now that she is not here , I don’t feel like baking just for myself ! Well go on , bake this cake and if you want to fill it with layers of ganache , please do ! it only gets more sinful . Go ahead you will love it .

The Chocolate Truffle Cake is my Day 7 post for Bakeathon . This week you shall see my bakes with chocolate .

    Chocolate Truffle Cake

    Ingredients


    1 cup flour
    1/2 tsp baking powder
    1 tsp baking soda
    4 1/2 tbsp cocoa powder
    3/4 cup sugar
    1/2 cup milk
    1/2 cup oil
    1/2 cup curd
    1/4 cup unsweetened coffee
    1tsp vanilla essence

    Frosting


    Chocolate Ganache

    Method


    Preheat the oven to 175 deg C.
    Grease and line a cake tin .
    Sift flour , sugar , cocoa , baking powder and baking soda .
    Mix oil , milk , curd and vanilla .
    Mix the dry ingredients with wet ingredients till well incorporated .
    Heat 1:4 cup of water and add 1/2 tsp of coffee .
    Add this coffee to the cake batter and mix well .
    At this point you can divide the cake between two tins . ( optional , I didn’t do )
    Bake for 30 -40 minutes or until a skewer comes out clean . ( this would depend on how thick your cake is .)
    Invert the cake on a cooling rack and wait for it to come to room temperature .
    You can now frost the cake .

    • Frosting

    • Spread the chocolate ganache on top and cover the sides too .
      I made a simple pattern with a fork .
      Chill and serve .
    • Notes


    In case you want a filling in between the cake then you should make two cakes or cut it into two .
    Spread a good layer of ganache on the first cake .
    Sandwich the cake with the other cake and frost with ganache .

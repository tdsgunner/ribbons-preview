Assamese Bora Saul Khichadi

May 2020 4 hr 30 minFestivals

Jump to recipe

Assamese Bora Saul Khichadi is a healthy , nutritious and delicious porridge consistency khichadi from Assam . The Khichadi is loaded with veggies .

Bora saul is a variety of glutinous rice found in Assam. It has an important role in  Assamese traditional occasions like Bihu , which is completely different from mainland India and on the other hand completely similar to those found in Southeast Asia.This rice is glutinous and is generally used in pithas , or as simply boiled rice .

I got a pack of the Bora Saul rice from my daughter’s friend who has launched some Organic products and you can read all about them here . These products are sold under the brand name of Taru. These products have very good quality and there is huge variety .

This Assamese Khichadi is made with Bora Saul rice and yellow lentils . Vegetables are a must in all my Khichadi ‘s as hubby likes more vegetables than rice and dal . Khichadis are one pot comfort meals , and the Bhoger Khichadi is served to the Gods . It’s specially made for Durga pooja . I adapted the recipe from here , where the blogger has used parboiled rice , but I used the Bora Saul rice and the Khichadi tasted amazing . I served the Khichadi with Methi Chaman .

Assamese Bora Saul Khichadi

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rice
  • 1/4 cup yellow moong daal
  • 1/2 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1/8 tsp red chilly powder
  • 1/8 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/8 tsp biryani masala
  • Salt to taste

Tempering

  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • Pinch asafoetida
  • Whole red chillies
  • Serve with chilly oil

Method

  • Soak rice for 4-5 hours.
  • Soak daal for 2 hours
  • Boil the vegetables with salt .
  • Cook rice in a pressure cooker for 5-6 whistles.
  • Mix in the daal and veggies , add all spices .
  • Pressure cook again for 2-3 whistles .
  • Open the pressure cooker after the pressure has released .

Tempering

Heat ghee , add mustard seeds , cumin seeds , asafoetida and whole red chillies . Pour over the Khichadi .

Drizzle some chilly garlic oil and serve .

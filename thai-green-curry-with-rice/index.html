Thai Curry

Green Curry Paste

  • 1 cup chopped coriander
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tbsp coriander seeds
  • 5 green chillies
  • 1/4 cup lemon grass
  • Blend lemon grass with 1/4 cup water.
  • Strain.
  • Discard lemon grass.
  • Combine rest of the ingredients and blend, using the lemon grass water.
  • Curry
  • 2 1/2 cups coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1/4 cup capsicum cubes
  • 1/4 cup mushroom slices
  • 1/4 cup baby corn, sliced
  • 1/2 cup cottage cheese / tofu
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • salt to taste
  • Parboil the peas, capsicum, corn and mushrooms.
  • Deep fry the tofu.
  • Heat oil in a wok, add the green curry paste.
  • Saute’ for a minute.
  • Add coconut milk.
  • Add the vegetables, tofu, sugar and salt and simmer till the veggies are done.
  • Serve hot with steamed or Garlic rice.
  • You could use any veggies that you like.
  • Garlic Rice
  • 1 cup rice, cooked with salt
  • 1 tsp garlic
  • 1 whole red chilly
  • Coriander leaves, finely chopped
  • Heat oil.
  • Add garlic and chopped red chilly.
  • Add rice.
  • Add coriander leaves.
  • Toss and serve hot.

Enjoy this steaming hot meal!!

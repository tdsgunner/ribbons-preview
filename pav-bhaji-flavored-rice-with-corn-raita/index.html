Pav Bhaji Flavored Rice with Corn Raita

July 2012

Jump to recipe

Rice forms an integral part of the life of all Indians.  While going through the varieties of rice, I was amazed.There are more than 40,000 varieties.I had heard of only a few, I stock only basmati rice as this is the rice that I have eaten since childhood so basmati tops the list in my cooking.Jasmine,sushi and arboiro are the trendy rice, or so I call them.These have been recently introduced to the Indian market.

Sticking to my love for basmati  I have  cooked  rice with pav bhaji masala and served  with corn raita.You could use  the variety of rice you like.

  • Rice
  • 1cup . rice, washed and soaked for 30 mins
  • Salt to taste
  • 1tsp .. ghee/oil
  • Boil the rice in a rice cooker or in a pan.

     Veggies

  • 2tbsp .. oil / butter
  • 1tsp . garlic, finely chopped
  • 1tsp . green chilly, finely chopped
  • 1tbsp . onion, finely chopped
  • 3tbsp . capsicum, red, yellow, green, finely chopped
  • 1 .carrot grated
  • 2tbsp . fresh boiled peas
  • 2tbsp . boiled corn
  • ½ tsp . pav bhaji masala
  • ¼ tsp red chilly pwd
  • Pinch turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • Heat oil.
  • Add garlic,onion and green chilly.Saute’.
  • Add capsicums and carrot. Saute’.
  • Add boiled peas and corn. Saute’
  • Add spices and boiled rice.
  • Mix well.Simmer  for 5mins ,covering the pan.
  • Serve hot with chilled corn raita.

  • Corn Raita
  • 1cup .. whisked curd
  • 2tbsp . boiled corn
  • 1tsp red capsicum, finely chopped
  • Fresh coriander leaves.
  • Green chilly finely chopped
  • Roasted cumin seed pwd
  • Salt to taste.
  • Mix the above ingredients, adjusting the flavors to your taste and chill.

Baking Tips

April 2015 Eggless

Jump to recipe

Day :19

Theme : Baking

Baking is a skill and surely can be mastered , how ever we need to follow a few essential rules which should be followed carefully.

  • Firstly we should choose a time when we can work without distraction.
  • Read the recipe carefully and get all the ingredients on the counter.
  • Weigh and prepare all ingredients before you start.
  • Sifting
  • Always sift the flours, there may be unwanted particles.
  • Make sure to sift with baking agents.
  • Sift at least twice, it incorporates air.

Ingredients

  • All ingredients should be at room temperature for best results.
  • Unit of measurement
  • Always follow the same units of measurement throughout the recipe.
  • Use either metric or imperial , but never a mixture of the two.
  • Adding Nuts
  • Always add nuts , raisins, chips etc at the end.
  • This will ovoid over mixing.
  • The tutti fruity should be mixed with a tsp of flour.
  • Oven
  • Always pre heat the oven for at least 10 -15 minutes.
  • Do not open the oven during the first half of baking.
  • It will hinder in the rising of the cake.
  • Check
  • Test your cakes for done ess while they are still in oven.
  • Always insert a toothpick in the center, it should come out clean.
  • The baking time given in each recipe can only be used as a guide, as every oven varies.
  • Check the cake approximately 5 minutes before the end of the baking time.
  • Cool
  • Always cool cakes in the pan for 10-15 minutes, before loosing the edges and inverting them.
  • Cut

For perfect and clean cuts, run a sharp knife through an open flame, or under hot water. Wipe the blade and reheat between cuts.

  • Chill
  • Chill the cake for half an hour before filling and frosting.
  • When applying multiple layers of frosting, refrigerate between applications.

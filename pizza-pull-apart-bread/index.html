Pizza Pull Apart Bread

March 2013 Eggless

Jump to recipe

I had been eyeing the pull apart breads since a long time, but somehow never had the courage to bake one myself. I think I should give credit to Gayathri for influencing me,  after seeing her amazing collection of breads, I was truly inspired.So here is a super soft bread which is more like a pizza .My hubby who avoids any kinds of breads gave me a green signal to go ahead and make JUST that bread for a future dinner.So for the second day of BM # 26 ,cooking with all purpose flour , relish this Pizza Bread.

Dough

  • 1 cup wheat flour
  • ½ cup all purpose flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup warm water
  • Mix both the flours, yeast, salt, sugar and 2 tbsp olive oil.
  • Add warm water and knead a soft dough.
  • Sprinkle some all purpose flour and knead the dough for about 10 minutes.
  • Place the dough in a greased bowl and cover it.
  • Leave it to rise for an hour.
  • Tomato Puree’
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 onion , finely chopped
  • 1 tsp garlic , finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp ready tomato puree’
  • ¼ cup tomato ketchup
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Blanch the tomatoes.
  • Peel and finely chop the tomatoes.
  • Heat olive oil.
  • Add bay leaf .
  • Add onions and garlic. Saute’.
  • Add tomato and simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  • Add ready puree’ and ketchup.
  • Add salt.
  • Let cool.

Grind to a paste

  • Other ingredients
  • Olives, sliced
  • Jalapenos, chopped
  • Grated mozzarella cheese
  • Herbs
  • Assembling
  • Roll the dough into a big round disc.
  • Apply the puree’.
  • Throw in some olives and jalapenos.
  • Sprinkle herbs and cheese.
  • Starting from front ,roll up the dough like a swiss roll, a little tightly.
  • Seal the edges with the help of water.
  • Cut the roll into even slices. Place the rolls in a cake tin in a circular pattern..
  • Leave it to rise for another 30 minutes.
  • Pre heat the oven at 200 deg and bake for 30 – 35 minutes.

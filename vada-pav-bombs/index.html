Vada Pav Bombs

January 2020 55 min

Jump to recipe

Vada Pav Bombs is a fusion version of the famous Vada Pav which is the most popular street food of Mumbai .

Vada Pav is a Maharashtrian street food , with a Indian bun , called Pav and a deep fried spicy potato fritter .  The Vada Pav is one of the cheapest streetfoods in Mumbai , just 10-12 bucks and yet makes a meal for most Mumbaites .In the fusion version of Vada Pav Bomb , I have not fried the vada , instead stuffed the pav with potato stuffing, coated the pav with batter and fried . The whole thing looks like a big bomb !

Well , these Vada Pav Bombs are similar to our bread pakoras , where we sandwich the bread with a stuffing and then fry the pakora . I added the chutney to the pav , so it gets a real good flavour . A few months back I was travelling to Nasik and they had a similar street food , called Pav Vada . The same concept but without the chutneys . I definitely prefer these bombs , as the chutneys give an ultimate flavour to these .

Vada Pav Bombs

Ingredients

Pav
Dry Garlic Chutney 
Green chutney 
Vada stuffing 
Batter

Vada Stuffing 

1 cup boiled mashed potatoes 
1 tbsp oil 
1 tsp mustard seeds 
1 tsp whole coriander seeds 
2 green chilly, chopped 
Pinch hing 
Curry leaves 
1/4 tsp red chilly powder 
1/4 tsp turmeric powder 
1/4 tsp dry mango powder 
1/4 tsp garam masala 
1 tbsp ginger garlic paste 
Salt to taste 

Batter

3/4 cup chick pea flour
Pinch baking soda
Salt to taste 

 Method

Vada Stuffing 

Heat oil. 
Add mustard , whole coriander seeds,asafoetida , green chilly and curry leaves. 
Add turmeric. 
Add mashed potatoes. 
Add red chilly powder,dry mango powder and garam masala. 
Add the ginger garlic paste. 
Add coriander. 
Mix well. 
Cool. 
Make balls. 

Batter

Combine all ingredients in a bowl.
Make batter using water. 
Keep aside.

Assembling the Vada Pav Bomb

Roughly tear the Vada from center, make sure you do not tear it through and through. 
Sprinkle dry garlic chutney on one side and green chutney on the other side. 
Place the vada stuffing in between , making it a sandwich. 
Dip each stuffed pav in this batter so that it coats all over. 
Deep fry to a golden brown. 
Cut into pieces and serve hot .

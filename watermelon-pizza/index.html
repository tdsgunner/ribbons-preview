Watermelon Pizza

August 2017 10 min

Jump to recipe

We start with a new week and a new theme . This weeks theme is Make your own pizza .

I am actually very fond of pizzas , come to think of it who isn’t ? I choose the theme but later realised that since I am off wheat and it’s products I will not be able to eat one . It’s not only wheat but no cheese too ! Now this gets little hard , right ? Actually No !

Since the requirement of the theme is Make your own Pizza , it gets simple and I made a pizza with watermelon . During summers loads of people sent these as wats app forwards and I was fascinated . There are so many versions of this watermelon pizza but I followed the Xigua Salad recipe , which is a great hit in our house .

Xigua is the Chinese name for watermelon .

It is one of the simplest yet very flavourful and delicious salads . A dash of sea salt , drizzle of honey and herbs like oregano and chilly flakes is all you need . Mango is the king of fruits it tastes fantastic in this salad and I somehow managed to get it in this season .

  • Few points to be noted when making this pizza .
  • One , always chill the fruits whole .
  • Two, Add salt and honey just before serving else they will start oozing water .

Watermelon Pizza

Ingredients

  • Watermelon
  • Mango
  • Onions
  • Capsicum
  • Oregano
  • Chilly flakes
  • Sea salt
  • Honey
  • Lemon juice

Method

  • Cut slices from watermelon.
  • Lay one slice flat on the serving plate.
  • Sprinkle onion, capsicum on it .
  • Now arrange mango pieces .
  • Sprinkle sea salt , oregano , chilly fakes.
  • Drizzle honey and lemon juice.
  • Enjoy your watermelon pizza.
  • Similarly make the rest .

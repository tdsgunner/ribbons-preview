Instant Corn Dhoklas

April 2011

Jump to recipe

I am always on a lookout to try steamed and low cal recipes,while going through the ‘march-april’2011 issue of ‘Cooking &more’ spotted instant corn dhoklas,tried them instantly.I did not make any changes in the recipe except that I steamed them in idli moulds instead of muffin moulds n garnished them with frozen peas n corn.

For this recipe I have used frozen corn.they turned out super soft and the taste was appreciated by all.Yet another hit recipe from my favourite cook Tarla Dalal.

  • 1cup .sweet corn kernels
  • ½ cup curds
  • 1cup .semolina(sooji)
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ tsp ginger-green chilly paste
  • 1tsp fruit salt (1 sachet of ENO)
  • ¼ tsp lemon juice
  • ¼ tsp oil for greasing
  • Combine the corn and curds and blend in a mixer till smooth.

Add sooji ,ginger chilly paste,salt and ½ cup water and mix well.just before steaming ,sprinkle the fruit salt and add the lemon juice over it,when bubbles form mix gently.

Pour spoonfuls of the batter immediately into greased moulds,steam for 15 mins or till done.

Cool and demould. 

I  added corn and peas at the time of steaming.so they don’t keep falling off!!

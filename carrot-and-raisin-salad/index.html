Carrot and Raisin Salad

April 2012

Jump to recipe

Summer has come in  and just the right time to eat  chilled salads! With Kayani announcing  the Magic Mingle and two wonderful ingredients..Carrot and Raisin..the very first thought that came to my mind was this Carrot and Raisin salad which is one of my favorite.We have a salad bar here which serves wonderful salads and this one is one of them!! So should we get on to the recipe?

  • 2 carrots grated finely
  • 1tsp mustard pwd
  • 4-6 tsp .. mayonnaise
  • 25 gms . golden raisins
  • 2-3 chopped dates
  • Pinch salt
  • Oranges  for serving
  • Mix the above ingredients .
  • Chop the head of oranges.
  • Scoop them.
  • Fill in the salad.
  • Chill for at least hour hour before serving .
  • Garnish with mayo and raisins.

Lemonade

May 2011

Jump to recipe
  • This is a classic summer cooler, loved by all. Very traditional and refreshing.different from our regular nimbupani.
  • Tried and tasted recipe from the book “100 Best Smoothies & Juices”
  • Here’s what you need …
  • 150 ml water
  • 1tsp grated lemon rind
  • 6tbsp .sugar.
  • 125 ml lemon juice
  • How to go about…
  • Put the water sugar and the grated lemon rind into a small sauce pan and bring to a boil.
  • Stir constantly.
  • Boil for 5 minutes.
  • Remove and cool to room temperature.
  • Stir in the lemon juice.
  • Transfer to a glass jug.
  • Cover with cling film and chill for 2 hours.
  • Serving style…
  • Take 2 glasses.
  • Rub the rim with lemon wedges.
  • Dip then in granulated sugar to frost.
  • Put  2-3 ice cubes in the glasses.
  • Pour the lemon syrup, top with sparkling water.
  • 1 part syrup and 3 parts water.
  • Stir well and  plunge in some lemon slices.

Garnishing

Garnish with a lemon or a sweet leme slice.

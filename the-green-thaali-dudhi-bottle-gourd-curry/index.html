I have already posted most of these recipes , so decided to post Dudhi today.

Dudhi or bottle gourd is a watery squash which is very healthy and nutritious . It is light and quick to prepare . I remember this being one of my favorite dishes as a kid . Mom used to save a bowl for dad who used to come home for lunch .Rest of the family normally finished before he came . While everybody would be resting I would sneak in the kitchen and finish that bowl !  Once mom realized that the bowl was finished by me, she did not mind but  hid another bowl for dad . Anyway let’s check the recipe of this simple veggie .

Dudhi ki Sabzi

Ingredients

500 gms bottle gourd
1 tbsp ghee
1 tsp cumin seeds
Pinch asafoetida
1 green chilly, finely chopped
1 small piece ginger , finely ground
Salt to taste
1/4 tsp turmeric
1/2 tsp coriander powder
1/4 tsp red chilly powder
2 tomatoes , puréed

Method

Peel the gourd , wash and chop in cubes.
Heat ghee in a pressure cooker.
Add cumin seeds and asafoetida.
As soon as the cumin seeds crack add the cubed gourd.
Add salt and turmeric powder.
Let cook on slow fire , water will ooze out but cook further till there is no water.
Add the rest of the spices and tomato purée.
Let all the moisture dry up . Once it is dry add about 1/4 glass of water.
Close the cooker and let cook for 2 whistles  and then simmer for 5 minutes.
Open the cooker once the steam has released .

The Green Thaali – Dudhi / Bottle Gourd Curry

Prep5 min
Cook10 min
Total15 min

Ingredients

Method

  1. Peel the gourd , wash and chop in cubes.
  2. Heat ghee in a pressure cooker.
  3. Add cumin seeds and asafoetida.
  4. As soon as the cumin seeds crack add the cubed gourd.
  5. Add salt and turmeric powder.
  6. Let cook on slow fire , water will ooze out but cook further till there is no water.
  7. Add the rest of the spices , green chilly , ginger and tomato purée.
  8. Let all the moisture dry up . Once it is dry add about 1/4 glass of water.
  9. Close the cooker and let cook for 2 whistles and then simmer for 5 minutes. Open the cooker once the steam has released .
  10. Open the cooker once the steam has released .

Baked Mexican Rice with Cheese Patty

May 2012

Jump to recipe

BM gives me a chance to try new dishes which normally I used to try when we hosted parties.With the changing lifestyles socialising has taken a back seat but I am glad with BM, my passion to try new dishes continues .Some how I have always fancied one pot meals. This mexican dish is really a very filling one with the goodness of lots of vegetables   enough protein and a perfect taste. The kids who avoid veggies get carried away with the cheese patty .The  mozarella lends it a wonderful cheesy taste that the children are fond of..some thing like a pizza taste with tomato cheese and olives!!

  • Rice
  • 1cup .. rice
  • 1 sliced onion
  • ½ cup mix vegetables
  • ¼ ..capsicum chopped
  • 1tsp chilly pwd
  • 1tsp soy sauce
  • Salt to taste
  • Heat oil / ghee in a pan.
  • Add sliced onions and capsicum. Saute’.
  • Add th rice and fry for a few minutes.
  • Add mix vegetables,salt ,,chilly pwd and soy sauce.Mix well.
  • Add 2 cups water.
  • Transfer to rice cooker.
  • Cook till done.

Tomato Puree
1 ..small onion
4-6 ..tomatoes
4 ..cloves garlic
1 bay leaf
2tbsp ready tomato puree
½ tsp red chilly pwd
Pinch sugar
Salt to taste
Chop onion and tomatoes roughly.
Heat 1tsp oil add bay leaf , garlic and onion.
Add chopped tomatoes.
Add 1 cup water. Cook till tomatoes are tender.
Cool  the mix.
Blend in a mixer.Strain.
Add ready tomato puree,salt,chilly pwd and a pinch sugar.
Cook for a few  minutes.

  • Cheese Patty
  • 2cups grated cottage cheese
  • ½ cup . boiled mashed potatoes
  • 1tbsp .. cooking cheese
  • 2tbsp .. corn flour
  • ¼ tsp red chilly pwd
  • Coriander leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Mix all ingredients and bind into round pattys.
  • Shallow  fry.
  • Keep aside.

Assembling
In a oven proof dish spread the prepared rice.
Place the pattys on this rice.
Pour the tomato puree’.
Sprinkle mozarella cheese.
Chop olives and place over pattys.
Bake in an oven at 450 deg for 15 minutes.

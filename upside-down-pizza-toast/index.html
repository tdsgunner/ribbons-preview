Upside Down Pizza Toast

February 2019 20 min

Jump to recipe

Upside Down Pizza Toast is a delicious toast with pizza flavours and makes a lovely filling snack .

When Valli announced the Upside Down Theme , I picked it without giving it a thought , then realised that there were so many cakes which fitted the theme . Upside Down Pineapple Cake topped the list , but I was looking for at least one savoury dish .

The Upside Down Pizza Toast is inspired by one of those quick videos on FB where they use egg to make this toast . Since I do not use eggs the vegetarian option was to use chickpea flour batter . I was not happy with the pictures and wanted to re do , but with my health conditions it’s not possible , so even though I know it’s not justice to the recipe , I am posting it . Those of you who eat eggs can make the toast by adding an egg over the onion tomato mix .

The toast is simple and quick and has a mild flavour of pizza and hence I named it Upside Down Pizza Toast . I have some pics for reference , hope you enjoy this toast which can make a great lunch box recipe too .

Upside Down Pizza Toast

Ingredients

3 slices bread
1 onion, finely chopped
1 tomato , finely chopped
1/2 capsicum , finely chopped
1 green chili , finely chopped
1 tbsp tomato ketchup
1/4 tsp pizza seasoning
Cheese slice

Batter

1/4 cup chickpea flour
Salt to taste

Method

Take chickpea flour in a mixing bowl .
Add salt .
Gradually add water and make a thin consistency lump free batter .
Take the bread and cut a rectangle / square leaving 1 “ on all sides . ( please refer the picture )
Heat a non stick griddle .
Drizzle some oil .
Add onions , tomatoes , capsicum, green chilly .
Sauté for a minute or two .
Add the ketchup , pizza seasoning and salt .
Divide the filling into 3 parts on the same griddle.
Place the hollow bread slices on top . ( Refer pics )
Pour some batter over this mixture which is inside this cavity.
Cover with cheese and then the bread piece that is saved from the square / rectangle that we had cut .
Drizzle little oil and flip over the pizza .
Let it toast and become slightly crisp .
Flip and serve .

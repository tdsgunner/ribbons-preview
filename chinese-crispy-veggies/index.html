Chinese Crispy Veggies

March 2013

Jump to recipe

So, I am back, and for the first week, I have picked up Pick one ingredient and make three dishes. To make it more challenging we have to do a set of combos, and I have picked Finger food, Bread and Sweet.

I shall cook with All-purpose flour for the coming three days.

For Day 1 it is Crispy Veggies. These are just like our Indian pakoras, just that the batter is different. Chinese Pakoras! If you may call them so. We serve these at our restaurant and they are a big hit with the guests. Spicy chilly garlic sauce is an absolute must to relish these.

Batter

  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • Pinch aji no moto
  • Pinch white pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • Mix all ingredients and make a soft batter adding water. (like pakoras)
  • Veggies
  • Capsicum
  • Cauliflower
  • Cucumber
  • Onion
  • Cabbage
  • Cut thin flat, broad slices from veggies, for cabbage just separate the leaves and cut broad squares.
  • How to go about
  • Heat oil for deep frying.
  • Dip the veggies one by one and deep fry till golden and crisp.
  • Serve them hot with chilly garlic sauce.

Chilly Garlic Sauce

  • 2 tbsp garlic, roughly chopped
  • 1 tbsp spring onion, finely chopped ( optional )
  • 1 tsp cornflour, dissolved in water
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1tsp red chilly sauce
  • 1tsp vinegar
  • Salt to taste
  • Heat 1 tsp oil.
  • Add garlic and spring onion.
  • Sauté’.
  • Add 1 cup water.
  • Add all ingredients, except the cornflour mix.
  • Let the water boil.
  • Slowly add the cornflour mix.
  • Cook for a minute or two.
  • Check the consistency, should not be very thick, as it shall become thick after cooling.

All veggies taste good, but I must recommend cabbage as a must, it tastes The Best!

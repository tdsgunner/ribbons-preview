Faces to Blog…Meet !! 25th BM Celebrations

March 2013

The US trip came to an end and I was back home only to realize that life gives you your share of joys and sorrows. My father who had been in bed since quite sometime passed away and I was totally shaken and could not accept the fact.The BM Meet dates were coming close , Valli called me to say they would cancel the whole event. Life has to move on , and I did not want to dishearten my friends, everyone had been looking forward to this event. It was Dad who taught me entertaining, so had he been there he would have asked me to get on with the plans, and so, I did .Lot of mails were exchanged, dress codes planned, itinerary’ s made, demos and workshops it was as though we were planning a wedding ! Throughout the planning  Pradnya was very helpful. I had the liberty to call her at odd hours too.

 Thanks Pradnya!..well, there is yet another  person who has to be thanked..and that’s my dear hubby he was the one to suggest the venue and decide the menus.Without him, I stand no where.

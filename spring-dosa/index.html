Spring Dosa

May 2015

Jump to recipe

My hubby is a hard core fan of South Indian cuisine and specially Dosas. Normally I make masala dosas and the masala is that of potatoes , but sometimes I find that very boring and prefer a Spring Dosa. Spring Dosas are very popular in Mumbai and it has a delicious stuffing. The stuffing includes, shredded cabbage, carrot , capsicum and coconut. The spices are moderate and somehow this is light on the tummy too.

 

 1 cup Dosa Batter

Stuffing

  • 1 onion , sliced
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • Pinch asafoetida
  • Few curry leaves
  • 1-4 tsp Bengal gram
  • 1/4 tsp white gram 
  • 2 whole red chillies, broken
  • 1 cup onion, chopped
  • 1 cup cabbage, shredded
  • 1/2 cup carrot, julienned
  • 1 /2 cup capsicum, julienned
  • Salt to taste
  • Heat oil.
  • Add mustard seeds, grams, asafoetida, curry leaves and whole red chillies.
  • Saute for a minute.
  • Add slit green chillies.
  • Add onions and capsicums. Saute.
  • Add cabbage and carrots.
  • Add salt and give it a good toss.
  • Switch off the gas and add coriander leaves and grated coconut.
  • Serve with Sambar and chutneys.

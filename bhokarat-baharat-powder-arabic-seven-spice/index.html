Bhokarat / Baharat Powder – Arabic Seven Spice

September 2014

Jump to recipe

The Arabic mixture is close to Garam Masala , but I do not like to substitute my spices. Just one spice can make a difference and change the complete aroma and flavor. This is a mix which is readily available at Middle Eastern stores. As of now it is available at some special stores here in India too. I decided to make the spice mix myself as I felt I could easily make it in no time, where I would have to spend a god two hours to go and buy one. It might be called 7 spice but I read many recipes which had more than eight spices too. This is a recipe that my cousin follows who spent a good time of her life in Middle East.

  • 2 tbsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tbsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tbsp cloves
  • 1/2 tbsp ginger powder
  • 1/2 tbsp green cardamon
  • Dry roast all ingredients, until they release a aroma.
  • Cool.
  • Blend to a fine powder and store in a air tight jar.
  • I used this spice in  Kibbe stuffing.

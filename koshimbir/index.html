Koshimbir

July 2018 55 minHigh Protein

Jump to recipe

Koshimbir is another name for a salad .This Koshimbir belongs to Udupi Cuisine . The salad is high in  protein and has a mix of raw lentils and veggies .

The lentil used in this Koshimbir is yellow moong daal which needs to be soaked for a few hours . The lentil should be soft yet crunchy . While making raw lentil salads one should make sure that you wash the lentils well a couple of times so that there is no raw after flavor . If it has been washed well and then soaked you just have to drain the excess water .

Once you drain the water leave it in the sieve for a while so that there’s no moisture . You can add the rest of the ingredients and seasonings . Chill the salad if you wish too , but remember cling wrap the bowl before putting it in the refrigerator .

I had prepared this Koshimbir for the Protein Rich Udupi Thali and we had enjoyed it thoroughly and my sincere thanks to PJ for helping me with this Thali .

Koshimbir

Ingredients

1/4 cup moong daal
1 cucumber , peeled and chopped
1 carrot , grated
1 tbsp raw mango , finely chopped
1 green chilly, chopped
1/4 tsp ginger , grated
1/2 tsp cumin powder
Lemon juice to taste
Salt to taste
Coriander leaves

Method

Wash the moong daal and soak till the daal is soft , yet has a crunch .
Mix in all the ingredients , adding salt and lemon juice too .
Mix well and garnish with coriander leaves.

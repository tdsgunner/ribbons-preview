 Envelopes of Love 

My passion for paper started when n I started working as a volunteer designer with a handicapped and blind girls institute. Amazingly  these girls are really talented. I  helped them with designing envelopes. The blind could not do much but girls with partial vision did great jobs . I was careful to keep my designing simple.

Most of the paper we used was handmade,we got the envelopes made by a local vendor . There after we embellished them . After finishing the envelopes were packed and sold at exhibitions .

These envelopes are popular as Lifafa which is the Hindi word for envelopes . One can put rupees or dollars in these decorative envelopes which are  used as gifts for special occasions like weddings , birthdays etc .

                                  envelopes made of fabric. These could not be done by the girls so my mom.in law helped.

These are small envelopes where a mini envelope is enclosed inside .

Moong Daal Paneer Cheela

May 2011

Jump to recipe

Another great recipe for breakfast,which is quick , low cal and nutritious is the Moong daal cheelas.These are nothing but savoury bean pancakes.

Moong daal cheelas are a speciality of Rajasthan.Normally they are made by simply  grinding the daal ,but I use paneer while grinding, this makes the cheelas really soft.Here goes the recipe…

Batter

  • 2cups yellow moong daal
  • 1cup ..grated paneer /tofu
  • Salt to taste
  • Wash and soak moong daal overnight.
  • Grind to a fine paste adding paneer.
  • Add salt.
  •  (batter consistency…dosa)
  • Stuffing / topping
  • 1cup .grated paneer / tofu
  • Finely chooped coriander
  • Finely chopped green chilly
  • Finely chopped spring onion
  • Salt to taste

Chaat masala

  • Mix all ingredients except chaat masala.
  • Heat a non stick gridle.
  • Spread the batter in circular motion
  • (similar to that of dosa)
  • Let cook sprinkling little oil.
  • Spread the paneer mix.

Sprinkle chaat masala

Flip over for just a few seconds.

 You are ready to serve.

These cheelas can be made uttapam style..that is with toppings , alternatively you can make these dosa style wherein you have to add the paneer stuffing.Serve with mint chutney.

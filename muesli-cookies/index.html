These Muesli cookies are from the batch of cookies that went to US for my friend’s grand kid . The cookies are simple and can be turned vegan as they have very little milk which can be substituted either with soy or almond milk . They have no butter and can be made with any cooking oil that you use . You could use any variety of muesli.

Museli Cookies

Ingredients

1 cup all purpose flour
1/2 cup wheat flour
1/4 tsp baking powder
1/3 cup sugar powdered
1/2 cup museli
Pinch salt
3 tbsp oil
2 tbsp milk
1 tsp vanilla essence

Method

Grind the museli coarsely.
Combine oil, milk, vanilla essence and sugar .
Combine flours , baking powder and salt.
Mix the wet ingredients to the dry , forming a dough .
Divide the dough into 10-12 balls.
Press gently over a greased and lined baking tray .
Bake for 10-12 minutes at 180 deg C.

Method

  6. Press gently over a greased and lined baking tray .

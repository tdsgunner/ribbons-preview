Grilled Potato Sandwich

January 2019 25 minKids' Favourites

Jump to recipe

Grilled Potato Sandwich is a sandwich made with grated potatoes and grilled to a perfect golden with a smear of butter .

When you get married and come to a new house you really don’t know the eating habits of a family , and this is exactly what happened with me . I came from a bread loving family where we ate a variety of sandwich toasts , but in my new family there were more of Parathas , Cheela type breakfast . So one day I asked my hubby ‘ Do you get bread in Ahemdabad ? “ . My mother in law laughed and got one and made a grated potato sandwich . This is one of the first grilled sandwich which I learnt from her and I am surprised that in all these years of blogging I haven’t posted this !

The sandwich is easy and quick to make and tastes awesome . Pack it up for your kids , like I used to do . Don’t forget to put a ketchup sachet in the box . The sandwich tastes awesome with it . I have used Indian spices here but you can use just salt and pepper and it tastes equally great . Go ahead try this one and let me know how you like it .

Grilled Potato Sandwich

Ingredients

4 slices bread
2 tbsp butter

Stuffing

2 large potatoes
1/4 tsp cumin seeds
2 green chilly ,finely chopped
Pinch turmeric powder
1/8 tsp garam masala
Salt to taste
Few coriander leaves

Method

Stuffing

Peel and wash and grate the potatoes .
Heat a nonstick pan .
Add 1 tbsp oil .
As soon as the oil is hot add cumin seeds .
Let crackle and add green chillies , sauté for a minute .
Add the grated potatoes, salt and turmeric powder .
Mix well and after a minute cover the pan and simmer the gas .
Let cook till done .
Add garam masala and coriander leaves .
Put the flame to high and sauté the mix .

How to make the sandwich

Divide the stuffing into two parts .
Take one part of the stuffing and spread on the bread slice.
Sandwich with the other slice .
Apply butter on the top of the sandwich and place it upside down in the grill toaster.
Now apply butter on the second surface and grill the sandwich till nice and crisp .

Vatana Bateta nu Paratho

March 2019 1 hr

Jump to recipe

Vatana Bateta nu Paratha is a stuffed flat bread made with a stuffing of peas .The dough is made with potatoes and flour .

Vatana is the Gujrati name for green peas and bateta is potatoes . Since I chose to give a Gujju name I decided to make the paratha following the typical Gujju method of making the stuffing . Whenever I have made parathas with peas I have always boiled them , but today I kept them raw . The Lilva Kachori is made by crushing the raw peas or fresh pigeon pea . I followed the same style. I pulsed the peas along with ginger , green chillies and mint . The mixture should be coarse . One needs to sauté the mix to get rid of the raw smell of peas . After seasoning cool the mixture . The dough is made by kneading in warm boiled potatoes and spices , this makes the parathas very soft .

The parathas should be roasted in ghee to get a lovely flavor . I served these with Gujrati Keri no Gadyo Aathano , Mint Chutney and buttermilk , the typical accompaniments of Gujrati cuisine .

Originally I had planned these parathas for the alphabet V, under the theme Flatbreads and More, but later made Vedhmi which is a sweet Gujju poli.However I am posting these parathas as my Day 3 post under Kids Delight , Travel Food . Parathas make excellent travel food , specially while travelling by road or train .

Vatana Bateta na Paratha

Ingredients

Dough

2 freshly boiled potatoes
1 cup wheat flour
1/4 tsp garam masala
1/4 tsp red chilly powder
Salt to taste

Stuffing

11/2 cups fresh green peas
2-3 green chillies
2 tbsp mint leaves
1/2 tsp ginger chopped
1 tsp cumin seeds
1/4 tsp garam masala
1/2 tsp lemon juice
Salt to taste

Method

Dough

Mash the potatoes while they are still warm .
Add the wheat flour , garam masala , chilly powder and salt .
Mix well and bind the dough using water .
Cover and keep aside till the stuffing is being prepared .

Stuffing

Using a mixer pulse fresh green peas , ginger , green chilly and mint leaves .
Heat 1 tbsp oil in a non stick pan .
Add cumin seeds , let crack .
Add the coarse peas mix .
Add salt , garam masala and lemon juice.
Sauté the mix till dry and well combined .
Let cool .

Making the Paratha

Pinch out lemon sized ball from the dough .
Roll using the dry wheat flour to a 4” disc .
Apply ghee to the disc and place about 2 tbsp stuffing .
Sprinkle some dry flour and bring the ends together to seal the ends .
Flatten lightly and pat with hands evenly to make a paratha.
Heat a griddle and cook the paratha till brown spots appear .
Flip over and let the other side cook too .
Smear lavishly with ghee on both sides and cook the rest of the parathas in the similar manner .

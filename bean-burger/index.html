Bean Burger

May 2011

Jump to recipe

All time favorite breakfast!!A traditional English breakfast of beans on toast has been Americanised with the burger.My children have always enjoyed these.I keep making variations in these. Sometimes I add small pieces of tofu or paneer in the stuffing…you cud even add some mushrooms and at times when I am short of time I use a mix different cheese instead of the cheese sauce.

The variations are many absolutely your choice!!
                                                                               

Stuffing

  • 1cup ..baked beans(ready tinned)
  • 2 tsp finely chopped garlic
  • 2tsp .finely chopped onion
  • 2tsp .chilly sauce
  • ¼ tsp oregano
  • ¼ tsp chilly flakes
  • Heat butter in a pan add garlic sauté till pink.
  • Add onions sauté till translucent.
  • Add baked beans ,sauce and the herbs.

Cheese sauce

  • 2tsp .butter
  • ½ tsp ..flour
  • ½ cup milk
  • ¼ cup .cheese
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper
  • Heat butter in a pan
  • Roast the flour till pink .
  • Add milk and cheese .
  • Add salt and pepper.

Garnishing

  • olives
  • jalapenoes
  • Assembling
  • Scoop out the buns.apply butter.

Fill up with beans stuffing

Pour some cheese sauce

Garnish

Microwave for 10 secs .

And yes those scooped bun pieces…don’t throw them…process them to bread crumbs.

Or if u have a dog..just a bit of tofu and these scooped pieces with some butter will be his special meal.(my dog is a fan of breads with butter.)

Ragda Pattice Mini Version

December 2014

Jump to recipe

Day 1 we have the theme where we were given a couple of menu cards and we were supposed to cook from it three dishes , different courses.I chose the Puranmal’s  menu.  From what I know Puran mal is a Sindhi into restaurant business. For Day 1 it is Ragda Pattice which was in their Chaat section.

Ragda Pattice has been one of the most relished chaats in our house. In fact at times we make it into a lunch meal. We even add a Pav to this, well you could check that version here. Coming to today’s version, I made mini Pattice as this is our starter from the menu.

I have made the ragda also with a different recipe which is simpler and quicker.

  • Pattice
  • 4 boiled potatoes
  • Salt
  • Turmeric powder
  • Coriander leaves
  • 1 tsp arrowroot powder
  • Peel the potatoes while they are still warm.
  • Mash and add all ingredients.
  • Mix well.
  • Make small balls and shape them into Pattice.
  • Shallow fry 
  • Ragda
  • 2 cup boiled white peas.

To be made into a paste

  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Small bunch coriander leaves
  • 1 tomato
  • Pinch turmeric powder 
  • 1/4 tsp red chilly powder 
  • Pinch Garam masla
  • Pinch black salt

Pinch chat masala

  • Heat oil.
  • Add the paste and cook till all the moisture evaporates.
  • Add the boiled peas along with the water.
  • Boil for a few minutes till well combined.
  • Assembling the Ragda Pattice
  • Place a tikki in a bowl.
  • Put a ladle ful of the ragda.
  • Drizzle garlic chutney, green chutney and sweet tamarind chutney,
  • Sprinkle nylon sev and coriander leaves.
  • Garnish with pomegranate pearls.
  • You could drizzle lemon juice if required.

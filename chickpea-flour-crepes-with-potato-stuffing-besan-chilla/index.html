Chickpea Flour Crepes with Potato Stuffing / Besan Chilla

November 2017 55 min

Jump to recipe

 I have chosen to work under the theme crepes for the first week of BM 82 . Yesterday I made Spinach and Lentil Crepes , today I have the Besan Chilla .

Besan chilla are very popular in Rajasthan , they are also very popular as a street food in Delhi . Now we have many versions of these chillas , the one available on the streets are very different and I shall definitely post those too . Today’s version is a routine breakfast chilla served in Sindhi homes … but with a fusion . The chilla is same but I added some vada stuffing to make it into a filling meal . We had these Chillas for dinner , though this could pass as an excellent evening snack . One can enjoy these without the stuffing for breakfast . So let’s check the recipe .

Chickpea flour Crepes with Potato Stuffing

Ingredients

Crepes

1 cup chick pea flour
1/2 onion , finely chopped
1-2 green chilly , finely chopped
Coriander leaves
1 cup water ( approximately )
Salt to taste

Stuffing

1 cup boiled mashed potatoes
1 tbsp oil
1 tsp mustard seeds
1 tsp whole coriander seeds
2 green chilly, chopped
Pinch hing
Curry leaves
1/4 tsp red chilly powder
1/4 tsp turmeric powder
1/4 tsp dry mango powder
1/4 tsp garam masala
1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
Salt to taste

Method

Crepes

Mix all ingredients and make the batter adding the water gradually .
The batter should be of dosa consistency .
Heat a griddle , drizzle some oil .
Take a ladleful of batter and spread in concentric circle , just like making dosa.
Simmer the flame and cover the chilla with a deep dish cover.
After 2-3 minutes drizzle oil and flip the chilla .
Cook the other side too drizzling little oil .

Stuffing

Heat oil.
Add mustard , whole coriander seeds,asafetida , green chilly and curry leaves.
Add turmeric.
Add mashed potatoes.
Add red chilly powder,dry mango powder and garam masala.
Add the ginger garlic paste.
Add coriander.
Mix well.

Potato & Onion Bread sticks

September 2016

Jump to recipe
  • Day : 13
  • Theme : One Category – Bread Sandwiches
  • Sub Theme – Sandwich Platters

Potato and Onion both are my favorite and I love these if they are baked.I like the crunchy golden onion slices . I normally bake these two and throw in some whole garlic and bake. Many a times this baked is our dinner along with a crisp crunchy toast. The seasoning is basic , yes oregano, chilly flakes, salt and pepper.

I love this combination, so I decided to use this as the topping for the Bread Stick Sandwiches.

For the spread I used pizza Cheese Spread ,Britannia brand, and I used cheese slices on top. Baked these and garnished with chives. 

The taste was undoubtedly amazing . 

So let me tell give you a recipe.

Potato & Onion Bread sticks

Ingredients

  • Bread slices, edges trimmed .
  • Brittanias pizza Cheese Spread 
  • Potato & Onion, bake
  • Cheese Slices
  • Chives
  • Bread Sticks
  • Cut the bread slices into equal sized strips.
  • Bake them till toasted.
  • Potato Onion Bake

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Onions
  • Garlic pods
  • Chilly flakes
  • Oregano
  • Black pepper
  • Sea salt

Method

  • Wash the potatoes and cut them into round slices.
  • Peel the onions and slice them.
  • Drizzle oil on a baking tray and place the slices.
  • Throw in some garlic pods.
  • Sprinkle salt and place the tray in the oven.
  • Bake at 200 deg C for about 30 minutes or till done.
  • How to assemble the sticks
  • Spread cheese spread on the sticks.
  • Place the baked potato and onion.
  • Cut the cheese slices into triangles and place on top.
  • Bake till cheese melts.
  • Garnish with chives.

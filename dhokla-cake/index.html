Dhokla Cake

November 2019 5 hr 10 min

Jump to recipe

Dhokla Cake is a steamed lentil cake made with two different layers of lentils and rice . It is soft and spongy and adorns your breakfast table .

Our family is a Dhokla lover family and many a times we enjoy these as a meal for dinner. Actually dhokla is served for breakfast or as a tea time snack , but we prefer them as our dinner . Every time I make these dhoklas , I want to try a new variation . This time it is a Dhokla Cake . I have used both white and yellow dhokla batters . I added some carrots to the yellow batter to make it more healthy . With rice ,  different lentils and veggies it was a complete meal .

It is best to make this in a cake tin where you pour the white batter first , let steam till done , then pour the yellow batter and steam again . One can even add a layer of chutney after the white batter has cooked . You can add more veggies too . Finally temper the Cake and serve with your favourite chutney .

Cut into wedges and enjoy !

Dhokla Cake

Ingredients

Yellow Dhokla batter
1 carrot , grated
White Dhokla batter

Tempering

2-3 tbsp oil
1 tsp mustard seeds
1 tsp sesame seeds
Few curry leaves
1-2 fresh green chilly
1-2 dry red chilly
1 tsp grated carrot
Coriander leaves

Method

Place water in a steamer and let the water boil.
Grease a cake tin.
Pour the white dhokla batter and place it to steam .
In the meantime add grated carrots to the yellow dhokla batter and mix well .
As soon as the white dhokla is done , which should take about 10 minutes , pour the yellow dhokla mix .
Cover and steam till done.
Test check with a skewer .
It should come out clean .

Tempering

Heat oil.
Add mustard seeds , let splutter .
Add curry leaves and slit green chillies .
Immediately add whole red chillies and sesame seeds .
Pour on the cake .
Garnish with coriander leaves.
Cut into wedges and serve .

Vegan Spinach Omelet

While browsing through breakfast recipes, I observed that egg was an important part of breakfast. Since we do not eat and get them I decided to make mock eggs. Todays recipe demamded an omelet, I made one but with chick pea flour.The Spinach Omelet is a part of the Iraqi Breakfast that I served for Buffet on Table.

Spinach Omelet

Serves : 1
Recipe Adapted from : Mr Breakfast

Ingredients

  • 250 gms spinach, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped garlic
  • 2 green chilly, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup chick pea flour
  • 1/4 tsp curry powder
  • 1/8 tsp red chilly powder
  • Salt to taste

Method

  • Wash and chop the spinach very fine.
  • Heat a tsp of oil in a non stick pan.
  • Add the garlic and let it turn pink.
  • Add green chilly and spinach.
  • Let the spinach wilt and let all the moisture dry up.
  • In a boxing bowl, add 1/2 cup chick pea flour, curry powder, red chilly powder, cooking soda and salt.
  • Add just enough water to make a soft batter.
  • The consistency should be like pancake batter.
  • Pour this batter over the cooked spinach and simmer the gas.
  • Let cook on one side, flip over and cook .
  • Drizzle oil while flipping.
  • Cut into pieces and serve.

We normally make these vegetarian omelets, but making it with spinach and garlic was new. It tasted really good and everyone appreciated as it had a health factor to it. People who eat eggs can proceed in the same way but instead of the chick pea batter add four beaten eggs.

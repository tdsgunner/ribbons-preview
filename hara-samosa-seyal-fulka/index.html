Hara Samosa Seyal Fulka

April 2011

Jump to recipe

Everyone likes to have nice hot chapattis  at  lunch or dinner but at times we do have some leftover chapattis,even though they r soft and can be used the next day…somehow reheating them dont like the idea and wasting no ways..so here is a super recipe to use your left over chapattis.Infact we all relish these ‘hara samosas’ so much that I make them from fresh chapattis.

  • 2cups fresh coriander(washed and chopped)
  • 2 .green chillies
  • 1 pod ..garlic(u cud use fresh green garlic in winters)
  • 1tsp ginger
  • 1 ..tomato finely chopped
  • ¼ tsp ..red chilly pwd
  • ¼ tsp ..dhania-jeera pwd
  • ¼ tsp . haldi
  • 2tsp .oil
  • Salt to taste

 Make a paste of the above ingredients.

Apply a generous layer

Fold into half,apply another layer

Fold into quarter.

In a nonstick pan smear a little oil n place these samosas, roast on slow fire till crisp on both sides.

ideal  breakfast  n tea-time snack!!

Ferrero Rocher Freak Shake Milk Shake

May 2017 10 min

Jump to recipe

Since the last two days I have been posting Summer Coolers. Day 1 was Gajjar ki Kaanji, Day 2 Mango Orange Lemonade, but today’s recipe is what takes away the cake in Summer Coolers. To be frank, I did not make this Freaky Milk Shake. My niece , Juhi made this late night  and called me . I did not know why she was calling , all I could hear was the excited voices of my grand kids.

The shake looked wow!.I was amazed at what these kids had created…they had called me so I could take pics of their creation .Due to the extreme heat and lot of impatience the shake needed to be captured quickly. The pictures have been taken within few seconds as the kids could not wait to grab the toppings .

Since I do not consume milk, I did not want to drink, but the kids made me have a few sips. and boy, was it sinful.I actually cannot find a better adjective, seriously.  Ferrero Rocher flavor was pretty strong, then there was Nutella, some Oreos. some random chocolates that they sneaked from my frig..Hershey, Lindt! A teaspoon of sugar, which could have been avoided…whipped cream laced the top and the shake was garnished with Oreo, Ferrero Rocher, Nutties , wafer rolls and finally drizzled with chocolate sauce.

Now come to think of it there is no particular recipe , but let me try and give you what the kids did.

Ferrero Rocher Freak Shake Milk Shake

Ingredients

  • 250 ml milk
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp cocoa powder
  • 4 Ferrero Rocher chocolates
  • 2 Lindt chocolates
  • 2-3 Hershey’s dark chocolate
  • 2-3 chocolate oreos
  • 1 big scoop chocolate ice cream

Toppings

  • Nutella
  • Sprinklers / Chocolate chips, both white and dark chocolate
  • Whipped cream
  • Nutties
  • Chocolate wafer roll

Chocolate sauce

Chocolate Vermecilli

Method

Let us prepare the jar first.

  • Take a bowl and put Nutella in it.
  • Invert the jar upside down so that the rim is covered with Nutella.
  • Stick some sprinklers and chocolate chips on the Nutella edges.
  • Spread some more Nutella on the outside and inside of the jar.

Now the shake

Add milk, cocoa, a large scoop of chocolate ice cream, Oreos,Ferrero Rocher, Lindt, and Hershey’s chocolates to a blender.

  • Blend till smooth.
  • Pour into the prepared jar.
  • Pipe a big swirl with whipped cream.

And the toppings

  • Top  with Oreos, Ferrero Rocher, wafer roll, Nutties or just about anything sinful.
  • Drizzle chocolate sauce and chocolate vermicelli.
  • Enjoy !

Notes: You can substitute the brand of chocolates, use what ever you have on hand.

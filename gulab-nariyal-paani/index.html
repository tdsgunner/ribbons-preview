Gulab Nariyal Paani

May 2019 5 min

Jump to recipe

Gulab Nariyal Paani is a refreshing drink made with the fresh water from the green coconuts . This tropical drink is further enhanced with rose petals and tender coconut meat.

We all know the benefits of the fresh coconut . It is known for its concentrations of electrolytes, vitamins, and minerals. The drink is low in calories and makes a wonderful sports drink . It rehydrates your body after a workout . You could read more on it . here

I love to serve this natural drink when I have guests . With the latest trend no one wants to indulge into soft drinks or sugary drinks . Coconut being naturally sweet is accepted very well , but if you give it a slight twist won’t it be a perfect drink to serve your guests ?

In this drink I have used the coconut water , a small part of coconut meat and given it a quick blend , just a few seconds . Add some rose water and some rose petals and serve . Trust me your guests will be more than pleased . The flavour and aroma of this coconut water is absolutely divine , heavenly .

I had made this while I made a Satvik Fariyali Thali , so presented it in a bowl to give it that rustic look but you could serve it in a nice glass and garnish with a rose bud , the drink will look exotic .

Gulab Nariyal Paani is my Day 1 post under the theme Let’s have a Toast !   

Gulab Nariyal Paani

Ingredients

1 glass water from fresh tender coconut
1/4 cup coconut meat
1 tsp rose water
Rose petals / rose buds to garnish
Sugar ( optional )

Method

Add coconut water and coconut meat to a blender and blitz for a second or two .
Add rose water and pour into a serving glass or bowl .
Garnish with rose petals .
If you feel the coconut is not sweet enough , you could add some sugar while blending.
Chill it in the refrigerator for 10-15 minutes .

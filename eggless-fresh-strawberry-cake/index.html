Eggless Fresh Strawberry Cake

April 2015 Eggless

Jump to recipe
BM # 51
Week : 2 , Day : 6
Theme : Baking
Theme of the week : Tea Cakes

Under tea cakes, today I have a strawberry cake.I used fresh strawberries with a wee bit of pink food color. Since I am a very very chocolaty person, baking with fruits was actually not my style, but after the cake came out of the oven it smelled heavenly. I could not wait to try. At the same time I wanted to make a dessert with this strawberry cake. Since the strawberries were in full bloom I made a simple dessert which was a total treat . I made only a small jar and it got over in minutes, something that I love. I hate when the dessert stays in the frig for more than a day.

  • I baked two cakes
  • I dusted the big one with icing sugar,  the smaller one I cut it up into slices .
  • I took a jar and placed one slice.
  • I layered it with whipped fresh cream.
  • Layered it with another slice of cake.
  • Next I added a layer of ganache, which is not very visible in the pics.
  • Topped again with some cream, and fresh sliced strawberries.
  • A strawberry trifle is ready.
  • Since this cake is made with fresh strawberries, it should be consumed soon.
  • Now coming to the cake recipe…

  • Fresh Strawberry Cake
  • 2 cups APF
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 3-4 tbsp castor sugar
  • 11/2 cups condensed milk
  • 250 gms strawberries
  • Few drops pink color
  • Sieve the flour with baking powder and baking soda.
  • Beat sugar and butter till creamy.
  • Add condensed milk .
  • Puree the strawberries, but make sure it’s not too smooth.
  • Add some food color.
  • Now mix all liquid ingredients.
  • Add the sifted flour to the liquid mix and mix well.
  • Add a few drops of strawberry extract.
  • Mix and pour into greased and lined mould.
  • Bake at 180 deg C for about 40 minutes or till a skewer comes out clean.

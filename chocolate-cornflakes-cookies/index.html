Chocolate Cornflakes Cookies

December 2017 23 minEggless

Jump to recipe

Bakeathon  is an annual event at Valli’s space where we have to post our bakes thrice a week . Every year I think of participating in this event but December is a very hectic month and I always opt out . With girls and kids coming for their annual vacation , I like to spend maximum time with them .

This year I had more than 15 bakes in my drafts and Valli knew about it , so told me to participate. And here I am . All these bakes were in my drafts since nearly 4-5 months . I had baked them for a friend who was travelling to US . Most of the bakes were done in big batches and we did three to four in one go .

For the very first day I have Chocolate Cornflakes Cookies .  I have used all purpose flour in these cookies but you can replace it with whole wheat flour , they come out equally good and The crunch from the cornflakes was excellent .

Chocolate Cornflakes Cookie

Ingredients

1/2 cup all purpose flour
1/2 cup sugar, powdered
2 tbsp cocoa powder
1 tsp instant coffee
1/4 tsp baking powder
1 tsp vanilla extract
1/4 cup unsalted butter
1 tbsp milk
1/2 cup crushed cornflakes

Method

Prepare a baking tray with parchment paper.
Preheat oven to 180 deg C.
Mix flour, cocoa, coffee and baking powder.
Beat butter and powdered sugar till light and creamy .
Add vanilla extract.
Add the flour mix to the beaten butter.
Add milk and form balls.
Roll on the crushed cornflakes and place them on parchment paper.
Make the rest of the cookies in the similar manner .
Bake for 18 – 20 minutes.
Let cool on a wire rack .
Store in a airtight container .

Xtremely Soft Nylon Khaman

October 2020 Festivals

Jump to recipe

Xtremely soft Nylon Khaman is a lentil cake which is prepared from Talod brand, of instant mixes. It is extremely easy to prepare this Khaman.

Gujarat is famous for its steamed snacks. Khaman is one of the most popular steamed snacks which is sold on the streets as well as Farsan shops. Farsan shops open as early as 7 a.m and one can see fresh Khaman, Dhoklas being steamed. Since they are very popular here, we normally like to get them from these shops. Of course, at times the family wants Homemade Khaman.

For making Nylon Khaman, we need special besan flour, which is the very fine variety, and many times we do not stock it. Sometime back a friend made Nylon Khaman and I was surprised, it was even better than what we get in the Farsan Shops. I naturally had to ask.

To my surprise, she told me it was an instant mix, the very next day I called for it and tried, it surely was a breeze to make it and tasted fabulous. Ever since we have stopped getting from Farsan Shops. I am sharing the recipe that they have given on the pack, which is super simple. The brand Talod is quite popular and I am sure now available all over the globe.

Xtremely Soft Nylon Khaman

Ingredients

Talod Instant Nylon Khaman

Method

Pour the whole flour from the Talod Nylon Khaman Instant Mix in a vessel.
Add 2.5 cups (360 ml.) water in it and prepare a soft paste and keep it aside for 5 minutes.
Take the contents of the small sachet in a 75ml. of water, dissolve it well and immediately add it to the paste, while stirring the mixture constantly till a smooth batter is formed.
Pour the paste properly in a plate/tin brushed with cooking oil.
Put this plate to steam the batter on high flame for about 20 minutes.
Remove the plate /tin and cool it for 5 minutes.
Heat 100 gms. oil in a small vessel and add mustard seeds and green chilli and allow it to splatter.
Pour two cups (300 ml.) of water in it. Boil it and sprinkle it on ready khaman.
Garnish with coriander leaves and freshly grated coconut.

Well, I haven’t been paid to write about this brand but it is just that I found this really really good and would recommend.

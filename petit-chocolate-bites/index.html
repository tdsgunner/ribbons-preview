Petit Chocolate Bites

March 2012

Jump to recipe

I have always liked chocolate or anything associated with it.I think it is one of the yummiest  and top favorite food of most people.Some time back when Rajani posted Home Made Chocolates I went ga ga over them but did not try them as have always avoided making sweets.Luckily for this Blog Hop,organised by Radhika I have been paired with Rajani..and I knew I have to make these choclates.I followed the recipe ditto..but added some hazelnuts to cut down the bitterness.Unfortunately I did not have the slab mould she has used so I made these into petit  bites.

  • 4tbsp drinking choclate
  • 4tbsp sugar pwd
  • 2tbsp cocoa pwd
  • 2tbsp milk pwd
  • 1tsp .. lemon juice
  • Water as required
  • Few hazelnuts coarsely crushed

  • Sift the dry ingredients to remove the lumps.
  • Add lemon juice and mix thoroughly.
  • Add water till you get a crumbly mixture.
  • Knead with hands to get a stiff dough.
  • Refrigerate for about 20 minutes.
  • Cut into rounds or put in a greased mould.Refrigerate for 5-6 hours.
  • Enjoy!

These turned out super cute and delicious and were over within minutes.Thanks Rajani for such a wonderful recipe.

Chocolate is actually a valuable energy source.It has great health benefits and helps in depression, high blood pressure so guys go ahead and indulge into this wonderful treat.

Coffee Muffins

September 2014 Eggless

Jump to recipe

The American Breakfast spread mostly has a muffin which is served along with the complete spread. So while serving this breakfast I decided to make these easy breezy coffee muffins. They were supposed to be vanilla ones, but for once there was no milk in the fridge…I myself was shocked, I did not have any milk powder too. Getting grocers or dairy products from outside means you are gone for an hour at least. I could see a big glass of cold coffee which was for my niece…I used the cold coffee instead of milk so the vanilla muffins got converted into coffee ones. I added some extra coffee and some chocolate chips too and baked them in a MW.

Voila! I was done with my muffins and what an awesome flavor. Here is a recipe for the muffins which are done in a jiffy and taste super.
  • Coffee Muffins
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 Tb sp coffee
  • Beat butter and sugar till creamy.
  • Add vinegar, vanilla and milk.
  • Sieve the flour, baking powder.
  • Add coffee to the flour mix.
  • Gradually add all the flour to the butter and sugar mix.
  • Mix till well blended.
  • Pour into moulds sprinkle some chocolate chips and microwave for 1 minute and 20 seconds.
  • Give the muffins a standing time of 2-3 minutes in the MW.

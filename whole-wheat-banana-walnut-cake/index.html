Whole Wheat Banana & Walnut Cake

December 2018 50 minEggless

Jump to recipe

Eggless Whole Wheat Banana and Walnut Cake is a flavorful , healthy cake made with ripe bananas and whole wheat flour . I have used walnuts for that crunch but you could add chocolate chips or raisins or even banana slices .

The cake is a complete treat and perfect for your morning breakfast or with a cup of evening coffee . The recipe uses organic flour and brown sugar . The cake is fluffy, moist, infused with sweet banana flavor, and loved by toddlers and adults .

Even if you are not a great fan of bananas , seriously you will love this one . It has real earthy flavours . You could substitute the brown sugar with honey or jaggery too . I took this cake for a friend who doesn’t bake but loves such healthy bakes . She simply loved it and so shall you .

The Whole Wheat Banana and Walnut cake is my Day 2 post for Bakeathon .So , should we check the recipe ?

Eggless Whole Wheat Banana and Walnut Cake

Ingredients

  • 3-4 ripe bananas
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 2/3 cup rice bran oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 11/2 cups wheat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • Pinch salt
  • Handful walnuts , chopped

Method

  • Preheat the oven at 180deg C.
  • Prepare a baking tin by lining and dusting .
  • Peel and mash the bananas .
  • Add sugar , oil and vanilla extract .
  • Mix well using a blender .
  • Sieve the flour , baking powder , baking soda and salt .
  • Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet , mixing well .
  • Add the chopped walnuts and mix again .
  • Pour into the cake tin and bake at 180deg C for 40-45 minutes or until a skewer comes out clean .

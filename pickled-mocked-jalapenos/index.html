Pickled …mocked Jalapenos

April 2011

Jump to recipe

Jalapenos  r a Mexican variety of chillies…really hot..a versatile ingredient…but here i m using an Indian variety of chilles…the mexican way inexpensive n gets done in a jiffy.use it as u r toppings in pizzas …or chop them n add to u r salads,subs..oh actually no end .infact I like to add it to my cabbage n onion toast with a bit of oregano n chilly flake…the cheese lovers do add the cheese to this ..its absolutely amazing!!

  • 250 gms ..chillies
  •  50 ml .white vinegar
  • 1tb sp salt
  • 1tb sp ..sugar
  • 2cups water
  • Wash n slice the chillies .
  • Boil the water add vinegar ,salt n sugar.
  • Add chillies
  • Switch off the gas.
  • Cover it …wen cool store in a airtight jar.
  • Stays good for 7-10 days outside…but 3months in frig. 

Hey guys dont get carried off by the jar lid!!they r truly homemade ..

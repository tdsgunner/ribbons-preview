Eggless Chocolate Cake microwave version

November 2017 20 minEggless

Jump to recipe

Under the last day under the theme Take Out Menu , I have a Gems loaded chocolate cake in a MW . When I picked this theme the first thing that came to my mind was a cake . We normally get cakes for most birthdays, of course unless I opt to make them .I had been without a MW for more than 3 years , well the family is totally against it , but I somehow felt that we need , specially when the girls come in . There is so much chaos with four little kids and four older kids . My daughters and son in laws are the older kids , naturally . They come once a year and need pampering and so get into the kids category .

Anyway when I got the new MW , the first thing I wanted to do was bake a cake . Fortunately it was my driver’s son’s birthday , so I grabbed this chance . Valli shared the recipe and I was quite pleased with the way cake turned out . I poured some ganache and decorated it with gems.The little boy was more than happy to get a cake , specially with so many gems.

Eggless MW cake with gems

Ingredients

11/2 cups flour
3 tbsp cocoa powder
1 cup milk
1 cup sugar
1 tbsp lemon juice
3 tbsp oil
1/4 tsp baking powder
1/2 tsp baking soda
Microwave safe bowl

Method

Grease a microwave safe bowl with either butter or oil.
Mix milk and sugar , mix till sugar is dissolved.
Add oil to this milk and sugar and mix.
Sieve the flour and cocoa powder a couple of times.
Gradually add the milk , oil mix.
Finally add the baking soda, baking powder and lemon juice.
Gently blend and pour into the prepared bowl.
MW the cake for 2 mins. Rest and then again MW for a 2 minutes.
Switch off and let it rest for 5 min. Let the cake cool.
Pour chocolate ganache over it.
Decorate with gems.

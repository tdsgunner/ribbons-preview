Kappa, a green from Uttarkhand

September 2015 Parties

Jump to recipe

This is a green curry that is served for lunch or dinner and is eaten with Bread, Pita, Roti & Boiled Rice. This is a dish, which is very sumptuous as well as nutritious since it is a special preparation of Spinach. This green is very popular in the hills of Uttarkhand.

Kappa
Recipe Source: Uttranchal Recipes

Ingredients

  • 500 gms spinach
  • 150 gms curd
  • 1/2 tsp chilly powder
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/4 tsp cumin seeds
  • Pinch asafetida
  • 1 tsp rice powder made into a paste with water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp ghee

Method

  • Wash the Spinach and chop it roughly and boil. 
  • Heat the Ghee add cumin seeds and asafetida. 
  • When seeds crackle, add chilly powder and turmeric powder. 
  • Fry the spices well. 
  • Add boiled spinach and cook a little. 
  • Add beaten curd.
  • Add the rice paste, stirring continuously.
  • Simmer and cook for a few minutes.

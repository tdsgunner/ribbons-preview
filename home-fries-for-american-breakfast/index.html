Home Fries for American Breakfast

September 2014

Jump to recipe

Throughout my US trip, I ate loads and loads of potatoes. Truly, at times being vegetarian can be difficult. While on my trip we stayed with family, so food was not a issue, but whenever we traveled and ate out, I felt potatoes were the safest. One can never ever go wrong with them.

These fries might be popular is US, but with me they have been popular ever since I was a kid. My kids too love these and we call them Haldi wale Aaloo , meaning Turmeric Potatoes, for the simple reason that I sprinkle turmeric and red chilly powder after frying them.

  • How to make these
  • Peel and slice the potatoes.
  • Apply salt and leave them for about 10 minutes.
  • Deep fry till done, you need not wait for them to get crisp.
  • Sprinkle turmeric powder and a pinch of red chilly powder.

  • I served these as part of the American Breakfast

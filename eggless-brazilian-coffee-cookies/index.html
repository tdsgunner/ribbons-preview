Eggless Brazilian Coffee Cookies

August 2012 Eggless

Jump to recipe

For the past two days I have been cooking with tea so  today I decided to make something with coffee. I simply love the aroma of coffee, though I do not drink it, yet I cannot resist coffee based products. I made these Brazilian Coffee Cookies, which turned out awesome, except that the ones at the back side of the tray were slightly over done. I scraped these and enjoyed them.

  • 5o gms ..brown sugar
  • 50 gms .. white sugar
  • 50 gms ..butter
  • ½ banana , mashed
  • ¼ tsp .. salt
  • ¼ tsp .. baking soda
  • 1tsp . coffee pwd
  • 1cup all purpose flour
  • 2tbsp .. milk.
  • Beat butter and sugars.
  • Add mashed banana and vanilla essence.
  • Add the flour and coffee.
  • Bind to a dough adding milk.
  • Place in a frig for about 10 mins.
  • Remove and shape into round , slightly flat balls.
  • Make intends with a fork, using some icing sugar.
  • Bake in a preheated oven for 20- 25 mins at 165deg.

These cookies had a distinct flavor of coffee, not very strong , but pleasing.They were crunchy , not one of those melting in the mouth kinds, but surely this crunch was what I was looking for.Some of the sugar crystals were still present, and these added to the crunchiness.Finally the indents with fork and icing sugar made them look really attractive.

Zero Oil Thali

March 2018

Jump to recipe

Zero Oil Thali is a spread of Indian dishes that are cooked with absolutely zero oil and yet it’s a great spread that tastes delicious. You don’t miss the oil or ghee.

For the last two days, I have been posting Thalis. Day 1, it was a Sindhi Thali, Day 2 it was a Rajasthani Thali and for Day 3 under the theme Thalis and Spreads I wanted to cook a Bengali Thali, I had kind of shortlisted the menu but then realized that the authentic Bangla cooking uses mustard oil. I simply love this flavor but my hubby just can’t take it. As a result, I gave up the idea of the BanglaThali. The search began for a fresh Thali. I pulled out my cookbooks and I saw a Zero Oil cookbook. Yes, this sounded interesting. I planned the menu spot on and revolved it around Rajma which was already planned for lunch. After I finished cooking this meal, I realized that we can cook most of the dishes without oil or fat.

Eating fresh is the key. Any freshly cooked food tastes wonderful, if it is zero oil then all the more. I finished cooking this entire thali in a little more than an hour with four gas burners and partly cooking in the microwave . Trust me zero Oil cooking tastes equally good and you cannot make out that the food has been cooked without oil . Above all eat your heart’s content and not feel guilty.

Anarkali Salad

Anarkali Salad is a healthy delicious and crunchy salad with a mix of fruits and vegetables. The salad gets its name Anarkali from pomegranate which is called anar in Hindi.

Vegetable Mint Kebabs 

 Vegetable Mint Kebabs are Kebabs made from Vegetables, mint, and nuts. They are made on a non-stick griddle and taste awesome even without a drop of oil.

Zero Oil Aloo Kandhari 

Zero oil  Aloo Kandhari is a rich gravy dish made with onion, cashews, poppy seeds, and potatoes. The name Kandhari comes from the Kandhari deep red variety of pomegranates used for the gravy. This dish got a 5-star rating from the family.

Zero Oil Rajma Masala

Zero Oil Rajma Masala is a kidney bean Indian curry, where the beans and spices have been cooked all together in a pressure cooker. Quick, simple, yet authentic, and delicious.

Zero Oil Gobhi Mattar

Zero Oil Gobhi Mattar is a dry side dish that tastes amazing. The ginger and green chilly garnish is the highlight of the dish and takes the dish to another level.

Fruit Custard

Fruit Custard is a no-frill dessert. The dessert is made with very little custard powder, to keep the dessert very light. Fresh fruits are added to give nutrition, color, and taste.

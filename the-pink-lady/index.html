The Pink Lady

May 2011

Jump to recipe

Water melon blended with strawberry crush and garnished with fresh cream .

Swirling the cream turns the drink into a pretty pink …hence “The Pink Lady’’!I tried this drink when two of my friends came over trust me they went all ga ga over it.

  • what you need 
  • 1 tsp strawberry crush
  • 250 ml…fresh water melon juice
  • 2-3 tsp fresh cream
  • how to go about
  • Chill the watermelon juice.
  • In a glass pour strawberry crush .
  • Add chilled watermelon juice.
  • Stir well.
Garnish with cream.

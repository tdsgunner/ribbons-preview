Hari Daal ke Kebab

January 2012

Jump to recipe

 I am not a T.V.fan but while I am in the office and have some free time I put on some cookery show and these kebabs are a result of one such cookery show.I , as usual scribbled the recipe on a paper and put it away and forgot about it..?…no..this time I did not make that mistake .I actually tried the recipe and cooked these wonderful Kebabs, which were  an absolute hit at home.I tried a new recipe for the chutney  to go with it.check out!!

Kebab dough

  • 1cup .. green split gram
  • 2-3 green chillies pounded
  • 1tsp . ginger garlic paste
  • ¼ tsp ..red chilly pwd
  • ¼ tsp ..garam masala
  • 2tbsp .daalia pwd
  • 2tbsp bread crumbs
  • ¼ cup . fresh corriander leaves
  • ½ tsp .. lemon juice 
  • Salt to taste

  • Soak the daal.
  • Wash and remove all the excess water.
  • Heat 2tsp oil and add the daal.
  • Roast till completely dry.
  • Cool.
  • Grind coarsely.
    Add all the ingredients.
  • Mix well.
  • Shape into kebabs.
  • Deep fry.

  • Chutney
  • 1cup coriander leaves
  • 6 . green chillies
  • ½ cup . besan farsaan (could be anything like sev,ganthia,papri)
  • Salt to taste
  • Lemon juice
  • Blend to a fine paste and you get a hot chutney.
  • Serve the kebabs with salad and chutney.

These are  also off to  Sweet Heat Chilli Challenge: Lets Rock Indian  and Susan’s  My Legume Love Affair # 43.

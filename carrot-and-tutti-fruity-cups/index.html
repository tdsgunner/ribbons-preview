Carrot and Tutti Fruity Cups

February 2012 Kids' Favourites

Jump to recipe

Thinking from kids point of view…I decided to make carrot rice…but Valli posted these ,so I wanted to make something else.Coconut rice ?…well you guys are all familiar with those, so no point!!…Whenever I have to cook something new I open the frig and keep staring at it.Keep shuffling things…and then  the beep rings…I hate that,but I shut it and reopen!! So today while shuffling I lay my hands on tutti fruity…and I knew what new rice I had to try.Carrots…tutti fruity …sweet rice.

 
  •  ½ cup rice washed and soaked
  • 1tsp . ghee
  • 1big . carrot grated
  • ¼ tsp .. illaichi pwd
  • 2tbsp .tutti fruiy(assorted colors)
  • 4tbsp . sugar(you can add or minus this according to taste)
  •  Heat a pan.
  • Add ghee.
  • Add carrots.Saute’.
  • Add rice. Saute’.
  • Add a little over 1cup of water.
  • Add illaichi pwd.
  • Let boil and then simmer .
  • When it is nearly done add sugar and tutti fruity.
  • Let simmer for 3-4 minutes or till done.

These taste awsum and the colorful tutti fruity looked so cute.You can surely make these for grown ups..add some raisins and nuts at simmer time.Let me tell you I loved cooking for kids and thoroughly enjoyed the different versions of rice.Hey friends…I wont say bye yet..I am coming back in the next group with some unique Kebab recipes,so do check ,even though you might not be in second group.

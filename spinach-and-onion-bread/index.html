Spinach and Onion Bread

April 2015 Eggless

Jump to recipe
  • Week 1,Day : 2
  • Theme : Baking
  • Theme of the week: Breads

Coming to the second day of this month long marathon, I have a bread which is very quick, easy and healthy. This is a bread which I had bookmarked long back from Gayathri’s blog.The bread is very flavorful and I served it with some cheese spread. I was shocked to see the bread disappearing in minutes. The onions, poppy seeds and the sesame seeds all lend it a very unique flavor. I was impressed with my self, if I may say so.

  • Spinach and Onion Bread
  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 cups onions
  • 250 gms spinach
  • 2-3 green chilly
  • 4-5 pods garlic
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • 1 1/2 tbsp poppy seeds
  • 1/2 cup oil + 1 tbsp +1 tbsp
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup curd
  • Heat 1 tbsp oil.
  • Add onions,garlic and green chilly, Saute till pink.
  • Add the chopped spinach, let it wilt.
  • Add poppy seeds and let them roast.
  • Let cool this mix.
  • Preheat oven to 175deg C.
  • Mix 1/2 cup oil, milk,and curd .
  • In another bowl mix together flour, baking powder and salt.
  • Add the flour mix and the sauted spinach mix.
  • Mix well.
  • Grease a 9×5″ loaf tin with oil.
  • Transfer the batter to the prepared tin.
  • Drizzle the 1 tbsp oil over the batter.
  • Sprinkle sesame seeds.
  • Bake the bread for about 50 minutes or till the skewer comes out clean.
    Let cool before slicing.

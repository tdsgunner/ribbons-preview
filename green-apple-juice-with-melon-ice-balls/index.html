Green Apple Juice with Melon Ice Balls

June 2019 10 min

Jump to recipe

Green Apple Juice with Melon Ice Balls is refreshing drink with these cute watermelon ice balls .

Watermelon is a fruit which is a must during summers . I love to drink it’s juice , mix it in mocktails or even make a pizza from it ! With the The No cook theme I wanted to make something from this fruit . I decided to scoop out melon balls and freeze to make ice cubes .

Normally  we should freeze the melon till rock solid , but here I have frozen them , but not rock solid . I wanted to add in the drink , but at the same time enjoy eating these balls . With absolutely frozen ones it would not be able to eat these right away.

The Green Apple Juice is a slightly tarty juice , and has a amazing flavour . When you make it at home obviously the colour is very very pale , so I have used store bought juice here. I chilled the juice and then added the melon balls . The drink is perfect to serve for parties or birthdays , tastes good , looks good and there is nothing that can fail in it.

After posting Nutella Shake and Oreo and Almond Carnival Shake this Green Apple with Melon Balls is my Day 3 post under No Cook Dish theme.

Green Apple juice with Melon Ice Balls

Ingredients

Green Apple Juice
Watermelon

Method

With a melon baller scoop out balls from a watermelon and freeze them lightly .
Pour the juice into glasses and add the melon ball ice to them .
Serve along with a tiny fork .

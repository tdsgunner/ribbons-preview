Nutella Shake with Nutella Cubes

June 2019 12 hr 5 minKids' Favourites

Jump to recipe

Nutella Shake is a sinful shake made with Nutella  cubes and milk . The Nutella cubes are made from that last bit in the jar .

There are no kids in the house and the adults well either they don’t like Nutella or some who like are conscious eaters . Result the jar comes only when the grandkids come . The regular Nutella stocking shall begin once Juhi is back after her masters . Masters ! Wow ! How time flies , it was only a few years back when I would entertain her school friends with burgers and pizzas .

Back to Nutella , I got a big jar for the grandkid’s as Nutella sandwich is their breakfast in Naani’s house . During this trip we could just do one painting project This wooden dog faced box ! This was made by my grand son It is fun when the kids come in , we have so much activity in the house, but after they leave it is an empty nest. Anyway, after  they left I realized almost 1/4 Nutella jar was left . I added a little warm milk and made ice cubes from the thick Nutella milk . With the no cook theme these Nutella cubes make a wonderful shake .

It is really simple to make the shake once you have the cubes ready , all you have to do is add the cubes to chilled milk , give a couple of stirs and you are good to go . For me the shakes look fantastic , but since I do not consume milk , I hardly make them, so this time I made and I gave it to the little girl who comes in to do my odd jobs . I wish I could have captured her reaction , she was over the moon . She kept rolling her eyes and saying – bahu saras che , mane bahu bhaye che . ( it’s really good and I really like it ). Truly speaking I was so happy and relieved at the same time . Relieved because now I can make these shakes without any fear , I have someone who likes them .

The Nutella Shake is my Day 1 post under the theme No Cook Dishes.

Nutella Shake

Ingredients

Nutella
Milk

Method

Add very little warm milk to the leftover Nutella jar , shake the bottle so that it blends well .
Pour the contents into a ice cube tray .
Freeze till this solidifies .
Chill the milk in the deep freeze till slightly frozen .
Add Nutella cubes to the chilled milk .
Give a few stirs and enjoy the shake .

Red Velvet Cookie with Vanilla Ice Cream

July 2017 4 hr 5 min

Jump to recipe

I made these candys while working on the Frozen treats ,where I had made Coconut Pomegranate Popsicles , Mango Coffee Popsicles and Anjeer Kulfi . I had made the red velvet cookies for my grandkids and the cookies turned out delicious . These cookies tempted me to make something interesting using them and thus the idea of ice cream with red velvet cookies came up .

I had made these candys while it was peak summer , so it was actually a treat to enjoy them , though a wrong time to click , but somehow managed . If you do not have the red velvet cookies you could use Oreos or for that matter any cookies that you like . These taste something like Cookies and Cream ice cream.
This is my post for the last day under Special Theme .

Red Velvet Cookies with Vanilla Ice Cream

Ingredients

Vanilla ice cream
Red velvet cookies

Method

Pulse the red velvet cookies till they resemble bread crumbs.
Soften the vanilla ice cream .
Fill half of your candy mould with ice cream .
Put 1 tbsp of the cookie crumble and freeze .
Remove after 2 hours add more cookie crumble and insert the ice cream stick .
Add more ice cream and finally top with more crumble.
Freeze again .

Baked Samosa

May 2012

Jump to recipe

Samosas the Indian stuffed savoury !!…deep fried to a golden.!!..crunchy.!!… lovely stuffing of spiced potatoes and a bit of farm fresh peas…wow!!..served hot with sweet and tangy tamarind chutney or fresh mint chutney…or would you prefer them with a nice cup of chai?  I looked  at these golden beauties ..something stopped me.Yes , you guessed right Deep Fried!! Thanks to Nupur Mehra of UK Rasoi who has come up with this new event “From Fried to Favourites”.

Trust me I had read many times about the baked version of these samosa but felt nah…not for me..they will never taste as good!!..but I was wrong!!..the baked version came out smashing hit.I tweaked the  bun samosa recipe which I had posted earlier, where the samosas were made with all purpose flour  and deep fried.The stuffing remains the same.

Samosa crust

  • 1cup maida
  • 1cup whole wheat flour
  • ½tsp warm oil
  • ½ tsp .. ajwain
  • Salt to taste
  • Sift the flour.add salt and ajwain.
  • Add oil.

Knead into a semi soft dough

Leave it for about 20 mins.

(this would make 6 to 8 samosas)

Stuffing

  • 2cups .boiled mashed potatoes
  • ½ cup ..boiled green peas
  • 3tbsp ..oil
  • 1tsp . jeera
  • ½ tsp .. red chilly pwd
  • ½ tsp ..aamchoor pwd
  • ¼ tsp .black pepper
  • ¼ tsp .garam masala
  • ¼ tsp . black salt
  • ¼ tsp . roasted jeera pwd
  • ¼ tsp . coriander seeds
  • ¼ cup coriander leaves
  • A handful of cashews and raisins(optional)
  • Mint leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Heat oil.
  • Add jeera .
  • Add all the spices and roast them for a few minutes .
  • Add potatoes and peas  and rest of the ingredients.
  • Mix well.
  • Cool.
  • How to go about
  • Roll out the dough in small thin round chapatis.
  • Cut them into half.
  • Make a cone.
  • Add the stuffing.close with the help of water.
    Brush the samosa with little oil.
  • Bake at 180 deg for about 20 mins turning them every 5 mins or till crisp and golden.

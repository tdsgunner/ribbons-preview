About

Hi, I’m Vaishali.

A homemaker and a food blogger. I live in Ahmedabad with my husband and the rest of my family, though I was born and brought up in New Delhi.

Before blogging I spent most of my time crafting and scrapbooking. I was also associated with children with special needs. Since crafting and scrapbooking take up a lot of time, I shifted to cooking, which was my passion too. Hence the blog’s name: Ribbons to Pastas.

What I love most is recreating food from every part of the world. When I travel, I like to meet the chefs, go to the local markets, and then feed my family and friends the dishes I’ve learnt.

This site is my attempt to document and share those recipes, along with family recipes passed on by close relatives. Try them yourself, and let me know how they turn out.

Happy cooking!
Love, Vaishali

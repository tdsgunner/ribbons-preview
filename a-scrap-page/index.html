A Scrap Page

April 2011

This is a scrap page that I did for my grand daughter’s friend.

I have used black textured paper to cover a plywood piece,so this remains firm

Since the pics were in deep pink I used purple (incidentally that’s her room color)to highlight the pictures.The gold dotted paper has been randomly torn n punched with a thread puncher to pass the pink organza tissue ribbon.

I have used my fav lollipop flowers in blending and contrasting colors,have edged the flowers with natural 3d fabric glitter liner.the leaves have been cut free hand and shimmered,from a pastel green gateway sheet.

I have used my cuttlebug for the butterflies..and for the letters…”adorable”..the doodles are also from cuttle bug.

Sticker frames have been used for framing the black and white pics.

  • the lollipop flowers
  • Cut circles in different sizes.
  • Simply crush them with hands
  • Open these.

Place one on top of other going from big to small adding glue in between.once done ,put a bling or pearl in the centre.glitter the edges.

try making these flowers with newspapers…brown paper…printed paper…just about any paper

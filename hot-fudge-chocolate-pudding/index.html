Chocolate Fudge Pudding Cake is a gooey pudding which is rich and sinful .A rich chocolate fudge sauce forms underneath a layer of chocolate cake while baking .


Chocolate Fudge Pudding Cake is yet another bake made by Juhi . She had a long list of the things she wanted to eat before leaving for London . Among that list was this gooey pudding . I remember making a similar pudding years back , but I seriously did not remember the recipe . She got the recipe and prepared everything . She called me at the last minute when she got into trouble ! She had used a smaller sized baking dish . I knew that the cake would rise and pop out , but then there was no option as she had already poured the water .


After the pudding was done we were glad it did not drop on the tray , it was like a mountain and both of us couldn’t wait to dig in . I quickly clicked the pics as Juhi didn’t want a cold pudding . My hubby kept giving us looks and felt I still behave like a kid . After the clicks we both attacked the pudding . what a lovely , warm , sinful pudding ! And so easy to prepare .


The recipe source said that the cocoa quality was important , and we decided to use Hershey’s . The pudding is ideally served with vanilla ice cream , but we both were quite happy with out it .

Hot Fudge Chocolate Pudding

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Fudge Sauce

  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp cocoa
  • 1 1/4 cups boiling water

Method

  • Preheat oven to 175 deg C .
  • Lightly grease a 9-inch square baking pan.
  • Pre-boil water in a kettle while preparing cake.
  • Cake
  • Combine 1/2 cup granulated sugar with the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder and salt.
  • Add in the milk, butter and vanilla; whisk until smooth and fairly lump free.
  • Pour the batter into prepared baking pan or dish and spread evenly.

Sauce

  • Combine together the remaining 1/2 cup white sugar, brown sugar and remaining 3 tablespoons cocoa powder.
  • Evenly sprinkle mixture over the batter.
  • Slowly pour the boiling water over the top (DO NOT STIR OR MIX INTO THE BATTER. ALLOW THE WATER TO SIT ON TOP).
  • Bake 35 minutes or until the centre is almost set.
  • Remove from oven.
  • Allow to cool for 15 minutes.
  • Serving Options
  • Serve with ice cream or whipped cream, and spoon fudge sauce from the bottom of pan over top of each serve of cake.

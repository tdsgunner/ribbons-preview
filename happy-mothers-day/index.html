Happy Mother’s Day

May 2011

It’s been ages since I made a card.Thanks to the ICR CHALLENGE which encouraged me to get my hand back on it.

Here I have used a digi image from google images,used water colors to paint it.

I have mounted the colored image on a muddy brown textured paper , have inked the edges with blue and pasted it on a deep bottle green cardstock.

  • Have embllished it with some punched leaves,ready flowers and hearts..not missing the organza tissue ribbon bow!
  • Have used the cuttlebug for’love’.
  • Mom’s unconditional love be it humans be it animals or birds it always there..it envelopes u!!
  • Love u maa .

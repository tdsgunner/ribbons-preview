Sri Lankan Cauliflower Frikadels

September 2014

Jump to recipe

While choosing my dishes for Sri Lankan Cuisine, Cauliflower Frikadels caught my attention. I chose my recipes from The Wonders of Vegetarian Cooking, these Frikadels are just like Indian Pakoras. The taste is very similar, though these are made by marinating the cauliflower with coconut and spices.  The batter too is different , but they are fried golden beauties which can’t be refused.

  • Check out how I made them.
  • Cauliflower Frikadels
  • 500 gms cauliflower florets
  • 1 onion
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1/2 tsp chilly ginger paste
  • 1/2 cup curd
  • 1/2 cup grated coconut
  • Salt to taste
  • 11/2 cups gram flour
  • 1/2 cup wheat flour
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp chilly ginger paste
  • Salt to taste
  • Make a thick batter with water with the above ingredients.
  • Boil the cauliflower till semi cooked.
  • Let cool.
  • Prepare a paste of onion, garlic and add the rest of the ingredients for marinade.
  • Marinade the cauliflower for 2 hours.
  • Heat oil.
  • Dip each cauliflower piece into the batter.
  • Deep fry till crisp and golden.
  • Recipe Source: Jyoti Nikunj Parekh
